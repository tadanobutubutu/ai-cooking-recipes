# Classic Tomato Soup

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Canned crushed tomatoes: 400 grams
- Onion (chopped): 100 grams
- Garlic (minced): 1 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Vegetable broth: 250 grams
- Heavy cream: 60 grams
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a pot over medium heat.
2. Add chopped onions and minced garlic. Sauté until onions are translucent.
3. Pour in crushed tomatoes and vegetable broth. Bring to a simmer.
4. Reduce heat to low and let it simmer for 20 minutes.
5. Stir in heavy cream, salt, and black pepper.
6. Blend the soup until smooth using an immersion blender.
7. Serve hot with a slice of toasted bread if desired.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when using the immersion blender with hot liquids to prevent burns.
