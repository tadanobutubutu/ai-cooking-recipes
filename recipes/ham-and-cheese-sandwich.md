# Ham and Cheese Sandwich

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 10 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Sliced bread: 150 grams
- Sliced ham: 100 grams
- Cheddar cheese: 50 grams
- Butter: 2 tbsp
- Mayonnaise: 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Spread mayonnaise evenly on one side of each piece of bread.
2. Layer the cheddar cheese and sliced ham between the pieces of bread, ensuring the mayonnaise is on the inside.
3. Spread butter evenly over the outer sides of the assembled sandwiches.
4. Heat a skillet over medium heat and carefully place the sandwiches in the pan.
5. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes on each side until the bread is golden brown and the cheese is melted.
6. Remove from the skillet, cut in half, and serve warm.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure the ham is properly refrigerated below 40°F (4°C) prior to use.
- Use caution when handling the hot skillet to prevent burns.
