# Hummus

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 10 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed: 400 g
- Tahini: 60 g
- Extra virgin olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Lemon juice: 2 tbsp
- Minced garlic: 1 tsp
- Cumin powder: 0.5 tsp
- Salt: 0.5 tsp
- Water: 2 tbsp

## Instructions
1. In a food processor, combine the tahini and lemon juice. Process for 1 minute until creamy.
2. Add the minced garlic, extra virgin olive oil, cumin, and salt to the food processor. Process for another 30 seconds.
3. Add half of the drained chickpeas to the food processor and blend for 1 minute.
4. Add the remaining chickpeas and process until the mixture is thick and smooth.
5. If the hummus is too thick, add water, 1 tablespoon at a time, blending until the desired consistency is reached.
6. Transfer the hummus to a serving bowl, drizzle with a little extra olive oil, and serve.

## Safety Notes
- Keep the hummus refrigerated in an airtight container and consume within 4 days.
