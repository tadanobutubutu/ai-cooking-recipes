# Cinnamon Rolls

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 60 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- All-purpose flour: 150 grams
- Granulated sugar: 30 grams
- Active dry yeast: 1 tsp
- Whole milk: 60 grams
- Unsalted butter (melted): 30 grams
- Egg (beaten): 25 grams
- Brown sugar: 50 grams
- Ground cinnamon: 1 tbsp
- Unsalted butter (softened): 30 grams
- Cream cheese (softened): 50 grams
- Powdered sugar: 30 grams

## Instructions
1. In a small bowl, warm the milk and stir in yeast. Let it sit for 5 minutes.
2. Mix the flour, granulated sugar, melted butter, beaten egg, and yeast mixture until a dough forms.
3. Knead the dough for 5 minutes, then let it rest in a warm place for 30 minutes.
4. Roll the dough into a rectangle on a floured surface.
5. Spread softened butter over the dough, then sprinkle evenly with brown sugar and cinnamon.
6. Roll the dough tightly into a log and cut into 4 equal slices.
7. Place the slices in a greased baking dish and let them rise for another 20 minutes.
8. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Bake the cinnamon rolls for 15-20 minutes until golden brown.
9. In a small bowl, whisk the cream cheese and powdered sugar together to make the frosting.
10. Spread the frosting over the warm cinnamon rolls before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Use oven mitts when removing the baking dish from the oven to prevent burns.
