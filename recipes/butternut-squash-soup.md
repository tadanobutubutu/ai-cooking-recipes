# Butternut Squash Soup

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 45 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Butternut squash (peeled and cubed): 400g
- Onion (chopped): 100g
- Vegetable broth: 500g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Heavy cream: 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add chopped onion and sauté until translucent.
2. Add the cubed butternut squash, vegetable broth, salt, and black pepper. Bring to a boil.
3. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 25-30 minutes, or until the squash is very tender.
4. Remove from heat and carefully blend the soup until smooth using an immersion blender.
5. Stir in the heavy cream and serve warm.

## Safety Notes
- When blending hot liquids, be careful of splattering which can cause burns. Let the soup cool slightly before blending.
