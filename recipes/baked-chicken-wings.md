# Baked Chicken Wings

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 45 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken wings (split at joints, tips removed): 500g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp
- Garlic powder: 1 tsp
- Paprika: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper or foil.
2. Pat the chicken wings completely dry with paper towels.
3. In a large bowl, toss the wings with olive oil, salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and paprika until evenly coated.
4. Arrange the wings in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet, ensuring they are not touching.
5. Bake for 40-45 minutes, turning halfway through, until the wings are golden brown and crispy.
6. Remove from the oven and let rest for 5 minutes before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
