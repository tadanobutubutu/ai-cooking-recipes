# Chicken Stir Fry

**⚠️ Allergens**: soy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken breast: 300g
- Soy sauce: 2 tbsp
- Sesame oil: 1 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Broccoli florets: 150g
- Bell pepper: 100g
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Ginger: 1 tsp
- Salt: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Slice the chicken breast into thin strips.
2. Mince the garlic and grate the ginger. Slice the bell pepper into thin strips.
3. In a bowl, toss the chicken strips with soy sauce and sesame oil.
4. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat.
5. Add the garlic and ginger, and sauté for 30 seconds until fragrant.
6. Add the marinated chicken and cook for 5-7 minutes until browned and cooked through.
7. Add the broccoli florets and bell pepper strips, cooking for another 5 minutes until vegetables are tender-crisp.
8. Season with salt and serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
