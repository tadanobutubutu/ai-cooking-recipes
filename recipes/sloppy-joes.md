# Sloppy Joes

**⚠️ Allergens**: gluten / soy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Ground Beef: 450g
- Onion: 1
- Ketchup: 240g
- Mustard: 1 tbsp
- Brown Sugar: 1 tbsp
- Hamburger Buns: 2

## Instructions
1. Brown the ground beef and chopped onion in a skillet over medium heat until meat is no longer pink. Drain excess fat.
2. Stir in ketchup, mustard, and brown sugar. Reduce heat and simmer for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
3. Serve the meat mixture warm on hamburger buns.

## Safety Notes
- Cook ground beef to an internal temperature of 160°F (71°C).
