# Classic Meatloaf

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / eggs
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 60 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Ground Beef: 450 grams
- Breadcrumbs: 50 grams
- Milk: 60 grams
- Onion (finely chopped): 50 grams
- Egg: 1 large
- Ketchup: 60 grams
- Worcestershire Sauce: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black Pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. In a small bowl, soak the breadcrumbs in milk for 5 minutes.
3. In a large bowl, gently mix the ground beef, soaked breadcrumbs, chopped onion, egg, Worcestershire sauce, salt, and black pepper until just combined.
4. Transfer the meat mixture to a baking sheet or a small loaf pan, shaping it into a loaf.
5. Spread the ketchup evenly over the top of the meatloaf.
6. Bake in the preheated oven for 45-50 minutes, or until the internal temperature reaches 160°F (71°C).
7. Let the meatloaf rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook ground beef to an internal temperature of 160°F (71°C).
