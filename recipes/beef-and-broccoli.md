# Beef and Broccoli

**⚠️ Allergens**: soy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Flank steak: 300g
- Broccoli florets: 250g
- Soy sauce: 3 tbsp
- Oyster sauce: 2 tbsp
- Brown sugar: 1 tbsp
- Cornstarch: 1 tbsp
- Sesame oil: 1 tsp
- Garlic: 2 cloves, minced
- Ginger: 1 tsp, minced
- Water: 60ml
- Vegetable oil: 2 tbsp

## Instructions
1. In a small bowl, whisk together soy sauce, oyster sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, cornstarch, garlic, ginger, and water. Set aside.
2. Slice the flank steak thinly against the grain.
3. Heat 1 tbsp of vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add the beef and cook until browned, about 3-4 minutes. Remove the beef and set aside.
4. Add the remaining 1 tbsp of vegetable oil to the skillet. Add the broccoli florets and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
5. Add 2 tbsp of water to the skillet, cover, and steam the broccoli for 2-3 minutes until tender-crisp.
6. Return the beef to the skillet. Pour the sauce mixture over the beef and broccoli.
7. Stir and cook for another 1-2 minutes until the sauce has thickened and coated everything evenly.
8. Serve immediately, optionally with rice.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure all utensils and cutting boards used for raw meat are thoroughly washed before being used for other ingredients.
