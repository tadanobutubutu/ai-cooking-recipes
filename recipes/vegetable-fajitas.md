# Vegetable Fajitas

**⚠️ Allergens**: gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- bell peppers (any color): 300 grams
- yellow onion: 150 grams
- olive oil: 1 tbsp
- chili powder: 1 tsp
- cumin: 1 tsp
- garlic powder: 1 tsp
- salt: 1 tsp
- flour tortillas: 120 grams

## Instructions
1. Slice the bell peppers and onion into thin strips.
2. In a small bowl, mix the chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, and salt to create a fajita seasoning.
3. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
4. Add the sliced vegetables to the skillet and sprinkle the seasoning mixture over them.
5. Cook the vegetables for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tender and slightly charred.
6. Warm the flour tortillas in the microwave or a separate dry skillet.
7. Serve the cooked vegetables in the warm tortillas.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when cooking with hot oil, as it can splatter and cause burns. Let the skillet heat gradually.
