# Coleslaw

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Cabbage, shredded: 200 grams
- Carrot, grated: 50 grams
- Mayonnaise: 3 tbsp
- Apple cider vinegar: 1 tbsp
- Sugar: 1 tsp
- Salt: 0.5 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.25 tsp

## Instructions
1. In a large bowl, combine the shredded cabbage and grated carrot.
2. In a smaller bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, apple cider vinegar, sugar, salt, and black pepper until smooth.
3. Pour the dressing over the cabbage and carrot mixture.
4. Toss well to combine, ensuring all vegetables are evenly coated.
5. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving to let flavors meld.

## Safety Notes
- Wash vegetables thoroughly before shredding and consuming raw.
