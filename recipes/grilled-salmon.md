# Grilled Salmon

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Salmon fillets: 300 grams
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp
- Lemon juice: 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat the grill to medium-high heat.
2. Rub the salmon fillets with olive oil, salt, and black pepper.
3. Place the salmon on the grill, skin side down, and cook for 6 minutes.
4. Flip the salmon carefully and cook for an additional 4 minutes.
5. Remove from the grill and drizzle with lemon juice before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook salmon to an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C).
