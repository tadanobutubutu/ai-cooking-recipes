# Lemon Herb Chicken

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Boneless skinless chicken breasts: 400g
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Lemon juice: 2 tbsp
- Garlic, minced: 2 tsp
- Dried oregano: 1 tsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. In a bowl, mix olive oil, lemon juice, minced garlic, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper.
2. Add the chicken breasts to the bowl and coat well with the marinade. Let it marinate for 10 minutes.
3. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
4. Place the chicken breasts in the skillet and cook for 6-8 minutes on each side, or until golden brown and cooked through.
5. Remove from heat and let it rest for 3 minutes before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
- Always wash hands thoroughly after handling raw chicken.
