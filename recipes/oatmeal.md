# Oatmeal

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 10 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Rolled oats: 100 grams
- Milk: 480 grams
- Salt: 1/4 tsp
- Honey: 1 tbsp
- Cinnamon: 1/2 tsp

## Instructions
1. In a medium saucepan, bring the milk and salt to a gentle boil over medium heat.
2. Stir in the rolled oats and reduce the heat to low.
3. Simmer uncovered for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the oats are soft and have absorbed most of the liquid.
4. Remove from heat and stir in the honey and cinnamon.
5. Let it stand for 2 minutes before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Watch the pot carefully when bringing the milk to a boil, as it can boil over quickly.
