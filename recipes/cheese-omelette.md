# Cheese Omelette

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 10 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Eggs: 4 large
- Butter: 1 tbsp
- Shredded cheddar cheese: 50g
- Salt: 1/4 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and whisk until the yolks and whites are fully combined.
2. Stir in the salt and black pepper.
3. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium-low heat and melt the butter, ensuring it coats the bottom of the pan.
4. Pour the whisked eggs into the skillet, letting them spread out evenly.
5. Cook for 2-3 minutes until the edges start to set. Gently push the edges towards the center with a spatula, tilting the pan to let the runny egg flow underneath.
6. Once the bottom is set but the top is still slightly soft, sprinkle the shredded cheddar cheese evenly over one half of the omelette.
7. Gently fold the other half of the omelette over the cheese using a spatula.
8. Cook for another 1-2 minutes until the cheese is melted, then slide the omelette onto a plate and cut it in half to serve.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure eggs are fresh and handled properly to avoid foodborne illness.
- Be careful when handling the hot skillet and melting butter to avoid burns.
