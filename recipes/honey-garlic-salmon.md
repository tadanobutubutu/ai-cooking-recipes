# Honey Garlic Salmon

**⚠️ Allergens**: soy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Salmon fillets: 400g
- Honey: 2 tbsp
- Soy sauce: 2 tbsp
- Garlic, minced: 1 tbsp
- Lemon juice: 1 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1/2 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. In a small bowl, whisk together the honey, soy sauce, minced garlic, and lemon juice.
2. Pat the salmon fillets dry with a paper towel and season with salt and black pepper.
3. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
4. Add the salmon fillets skin-side up and cook for 4-5 minutes until golden brown.
5. Carefully flip the salmon fillets.
6. Pour the honey garlic sauce into the skillet.
7. Cook for another 3-4 minutes, basting the salmon with the sauce, until the fish flakes easily with a fork.
8. Remove from heat and serve warm, drizzled with the sauce from the skillet.

## Safety Notes
- Cook salmon to an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C).
