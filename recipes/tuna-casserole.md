# Tuna Casserole

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 35 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Egg noodles: 150 grams
- Canned tuna (drained): 140 grams
- Cream of mushroom soup: 150 grams
- Frozen peas: 75 grams
- Cheddar cheese (shredded): 50 grams
- Milk: 4 tbsp
- Salt: 0.5 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.25 tsp
- Breadcrumbs: 30 grams

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 190°C.
2. Boil a pot of water and cook egg noodles for 6 minutes, then drain.
3. In a large bowl, mix the cooked noodles, drained tuna, cream of mushroom soup, peas, milk, salt, and black pepper.
4. Transfer the mixture to a baking dish.
5. Top with shredded cheddar cheese and breadcrumbs.
6. Bake for 20 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
7. Let it cool for 5 minutes before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure the casserole is heated thoroughly to at least 75°C in the center.
