# Chicken Katsu

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Boneless skinless chicken breasts: 2 (about 200g each)
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp
- All-purpose flour: 30g
- Egg: 1
- Panko breadcrumbs: 100g
- Vegetable oil: 500ml

## Instructions
1. Pound the chicken breasts to an even 1/2-inch thickness.
2. Season both sides of the chicken with salt and pepper.
3. Set up a breading station with three shallow dishes: one for flour, one for a beaten egg, and one for panko breadcrumbs.
4. Coat each chicken breast in flour, shaking off any excess. Dip into the egg, then firmly press into the panko until fully coated.
5. Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until it reaches 350°F (175°C).
6. Carefully place the breaded chicken into the hot oil and fry for 3-4 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through.
7. Transfer the chicken to a paper towel-lined plate to drain excess oil before slicing and serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
- Use caution when frying with hot oil to prevent splatters and burns.
