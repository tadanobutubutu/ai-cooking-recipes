# Lemon Butter Chicken

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken breast: 400 grams
- Butter: 3 tbsp
- Lemon juice: 2 tbsp
- Garlic powder: 1 tsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Melt butter in a large skillet over medium heat.
2. Season chicken breasts with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
3. Cook the chicken for 6-8 minutes on each side until golden brown.
4. Pour lemon juice over the chicken and simmer for 2 minutes.
5. Serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
