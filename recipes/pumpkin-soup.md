# Pumpkin Soup

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Pumpkin puree: 400 grams
- Vegetable broth: 240 grams
- Onion: 100 grams
- Garlic: 10 grams
- Heavy cream: 120 grams
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Chop the onion and mince the garlic.
2. Heat olive oil in a pot over medium heat, then sauté the onion and garlic until soft.
3. Add the pumpkin puree, vegetable broth, salt, and black pepper. Stir well.
4. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 15 minutes.
5. Stir in the heavy cream and cook for another 5 minutes until heated through.
6. Serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling hot liquids to avoid burns.
