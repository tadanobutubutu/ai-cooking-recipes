# BBQ Chicken Pizza

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Pizza Dough: 300 grams
- BBQ Sauce: 4 tbsp
- Cooked Chicken Breast, shredded: 150 grams
- Red Onion, thinly sliced: 50 grams
- Mozzarella Cheese, shredded: 100 grams
- Fresh Cilantro, chopped: 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C).
2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface.
3. Spread the BBQ sauce evenly over the dough.
4. Distribute the shredded chicken and red onion slices on top.
5. Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese evenly.
6. Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
7. Garnish with fresh cilantro before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Be cautious when handling hot pans and using the high-temperature oven. Ensure the cooked chicken was stored safely and is heated thoroughly.
