# BLT Sandwich

**⚠️ Allergens**: gluten / eggs
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Bacon: 150g
- Lettuce: 50g
- Tomato: 150g
- Bread: 100g
- Mayonnaise: 2 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Cook the bacon in a skillet over medium heat until crispy, about 8-10 minutes. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate.
2. Toast the bread slices to your desired level of crispiness.
3. Slice the tomato into even rounds.
4. Spread mayonnaise evenly on one side of each toasted bread slice.
5. Layer the lettuce, tomato slices, and cooked bacon on two of the bread slices.
6. Top with the remaining two slices of bread, mayonnaise side down.
7. Cut the sandwiches in half and serve immediately.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure the bacon is fully cooked before eating.
