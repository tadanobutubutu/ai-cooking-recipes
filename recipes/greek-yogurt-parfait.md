# Greek Yogurt Parfait

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 10 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Plain Greek yogurt: 400 gram
- Honey: 2 tbsp
- Granola: 100 gram
- Fresh berries (mixed): 200 gram

## Instructions
1. In two glasses or bowls, spoon a layer of Greek yogurt at the bottom.
2. Drizzle a little honey over the yogurt.
3. Add a layer of fresh mixed berries.
4. Add a layer of granola.
5. Repeat the layers (yogurt, honey, berries, granola) until all ingredients are used up.
6. Serve immediately and enjoy!

## Safety Notes
- Wash fresh berries thoroughly before using.
