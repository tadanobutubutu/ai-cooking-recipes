# Vegetable Curry

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Coconut oil: 1 tbsp
- Onion, chopped: 150g
- Garlic, minced: 2 cloves
- Ginger, minced: 1 tbsp
- Curry powder: 2 tbsp
- Coconut milk (canned): 400g
- Vegetable broth: 240g
- Potatoes, diced: 200g
- Carrots, sliced: 150g
- Broccoli florets: 150g
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Heat coconut oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Add chopped onion, garlic, and ginger. Sauté for 5 minutes until onions are translucent.
3. Stir in the curry powder and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Pour in coconut milk and vegetable broth, stirring to combine.
5. Add the diced potatoes and sliced carrots. Bring to a simmer, then cover and cook for 15 minutes.
6. Stir in the broccoli florets, salt, and pepper. Cover and cook for an additional 5 minutes or until all vegetables are tender.
7. Serve hot, optionally with rice or flatbread.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling hot liquids to avoid burns.
- Wash all vegetables thoroughly before chopping.
