# Hash Browns

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Potatoes: 400 grams
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Wash and peel the potatoes.
2. Grate the potatoes using a box grater.
3. Squeeze the excess moisture from the grated potatoes using a clean kitchen towel.
4. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
5. Spread the grated potatoes evenly in the skillet and press down lightly with a spatula.
6. Season with salt and black pepper.
7. Cook for 5-7 minutes until the bottom is golden brown and crispy.
8. Flip the hash browns and cook for another 5-7 minutes until the other side is crispy.
9. Serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when flipping the hash browns to avoid splashing hot oil.
