# Pasta Salad

**⚠️ Allergens**: gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- rotini pasta: 200g
- cherry tomatoes: 100g
- cucumber: 1/2
- black olives: 50g
- olive oil: 2 tbsp
- lemon juice: 1 tbsp
- dried oregano: 1 tsp
- salt: 1/2 tsp
- black pepper: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. Boil a large pot of salted water and cook the pasta according to package instructions until al dente. Drain and rinse with cold water to cool.
2. Halve the cherry tomatoes, dice the cucumber, and slice the black olives.
3. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper to make the dressing.
4. In a large serving bowl, combine the cooled pasta, tomatoes, cucumber, and olives.
5. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss well to combine.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when pouring boiling water and handling the hot pot.
