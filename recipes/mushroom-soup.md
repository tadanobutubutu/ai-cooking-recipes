# Mushroom Soup

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- button mushrooms (sliced): 250g
- onion (chopped): 150g
- garlic (minced): 1 tbsp
- butter: 2 tbsp
- vegetable broth: 500g
- heavy cream: 100g
- salt: 1 tsp
- black pepper: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and minced garlic, and sauté until softened, about 5 minutes.
2. Add the sliced mushrooms to the pot and cook until they release their juices and brown slightly, about 8 minutes.
3. Pour in the vegetable broth, salt, and black pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 15 minutes.
4. Carefully blend the soup until smooth using an immersion blender or by transferring it to a standard blender.
5. Stir in the heavy cream and gently reheat for 2 minutes over low heat (do not let it boil).
6. Serve hot, garnished with extra fresh herbs if desired.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when blending hot liquids to avoid burns. Let it cool slightly before blending if using a standard blender.
