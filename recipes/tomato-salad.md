# Tomato Salad

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 10 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Cherry Tomatoes: 250 grams
- Red Onion: 50 grams
- Fresh Basil: 2 tbsp
- Olive Oil: 1 tbsp
- Balsamic Vinegar: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 0.5 tsp
- Black Pepper: 0.25 tsp

## Instructions
1. Halve the cherry tomatoes and thinly slice the red onion.
2. In a bowl, toss the tomatoes, onion, and fresh basil leaves together.
3. Drizzle with olive oil and balsamic vinegar.
4. Season with salt and black pepper.
5. Toss well to combine and serve immediately or chill before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Wash produce thoroughly before consuming.
