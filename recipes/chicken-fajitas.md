# Chicken Fajitas

**⚠️ Allergens**: gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken breast: 300g
- Bell peppers: 200g
- Onion: 100g
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Fajita seasoning: 2 tbsp
- Lime juice: 1 tbsp
- Flour tortillas: 150g

## Instructions
1. Slice the chicken breast, bell peppers, and onion into thin strips.
2. In a bowl, toss the chicken strips with 1 tbsp olive oil, fajita seasoning, and lime juice.
3. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
4. Add the chicken to the skillet and cook for 5-7 minutes until browned and cooked through, then remove from the skillet and set aside.
5. In the same skillet, add the sliced bell peppers and onion. Sauté for 5 minutes until softened and slightly charred.
6. Return the chicken to the skillet and toss everything together for 1 minute.
7. Warm the flour tortillas in a microwave or dry skillet.
8. Serve the chicken and vegetable mixture warm inside the tortillas.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
