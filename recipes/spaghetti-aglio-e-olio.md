# Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

**⚠️ Allergens**: gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Spaghetti: 200g
- Garlic cloves, thinly sliced: 4
- Extra virgin olive oil: 4 tbsp
- Red pepper flakes: 1/2 tsp
- Fresh parsley, chopped: 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp (for pasta water)
- Water: 2 liters (for boiling pasta)

## Instructions
1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the spaghetti and cook until al dente, according to package instructions.
2. While the pasta is cooking, heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat.
3. Add the sliced garlic and cook gently for 2-3 minutes, stirring frequently, until it turns slightly golden but not brown.
4. Stir in the red pepper flakes and cook for another 30 seconds to release the flavors into the oil.
5. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water, then drain the spaghetti.
6. Add the drained spaghetti to the skillet with the garlic and oil. Toss well to coat.
7. If the pasta seems dry, add a splash of the reserved pasta water and toss until a light sauce forms.
8. Remove from heat, stir in the chopped fresh parsley, and serve immediately.

## Safety Notes
- Handle hot oil carefully to avoid splatters when adding ingredients.
