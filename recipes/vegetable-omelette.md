# Vegetable Omelette

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Eggs: 4 large
- Milk: 2 tbsp
- Butter: 1 tbsp
- Bell pepper (diced): 50g
- Onion (diced): 50g
- Spinach: 30g
- Cheddar cheese (shredded): 40g
- Salt: 0.5 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.25 tsp

## Instructions
1. In a bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, salt, and black pepper until well combined.
2. Melt the butter in a non-stick skillet over medium heat.
3. Add the diced bell pepper and onion to the skillet. Sauté for 3-4 minutes until softened.
4. Add the spinach and cook for 1 minute until wilted.
5. Pour the egg mixture over the vegetables in the skillet.
6. Cook without stirring for 2-3 minutes until the edges start to set. Gently lift the edges with a spatula and tilt the pan to let uncooked egg flow underneath.
7. Sprinkle the shredded cheddar cheese over one half of the omelette.
8. Carefully fold the other half over the cheese. Cook for another 1-2 minutes until the cheese is melted and the eggs are fully cooked.
9. Slide the omelette onto a cutting board, cut in half, and serve immediately.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure eggs are fully cooked to reduce the risk of foodborne illness.
