# Teriyaki Salmon

**⚠️ Allergens**: soy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Salmon fillets: 300g
- Soy sauce: 3 tbsp
- Brown sugar: 2 tbsp
- Minced garlic: 1 tsp
- Grated ginger: 1 tsp
- Cooking oil: 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. In a small bowl, whisk together soy sauce, brown sugar, minced garlic, and grated ginger.
2. Heat cooking oil in a pan over medium heat. Place salmon fillets skin-side down and cook for 4-5 minutes.
3. Flip the salmon, pour the sauce mixture into the pan.
4. Cook for another 3-4 minutes, spooning the sauce over the salmon as it thickens and glazes the fish.
5. Remove from heat and serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Cook salmon to an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C).
