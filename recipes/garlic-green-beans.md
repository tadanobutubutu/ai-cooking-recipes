# Garlic Green Beans

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Green beans (trimmed): 300 grams
- Unsalted butter: 2 tbsp
- Garlic (minced): 1 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil and butter in a large skillet over medium heat until the butter is melted.
2. Add the minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add the trimmed green beans to the skillet and toss to coat with the garlic butter.
4. Cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the green beans are tender-crisp.
5. Season with salt and black pepper, and serve warm.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful not to burn the garlic, as it can turn bitter. Lower the heat if necessary.
