# Pad Thai

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / gluten / nuts / soy / shellfish
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Rice noodles: 200g
- Chicken breast (sliced): 200g
- Eggs: 2
- Vegetable oil: 2 tbsp
- Pad Thai sauce: 4 tbsp
- Bean sprouts: 100g
- Unsalted peanuts (crushed): 3 tbsp
- Green onions (chopped): 2 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Soak the rice noodles in warm water for 20 minutes until pliable, then drain.
2. Heat 1 tbsp of vegetable oil in a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the sliced chicken and cook until browned and cooked through.
3. Push the chicken to one side of the pan, add the remaining 1 tbsp of oil, and crack the eggs into the pan. Scramble the eggs until just set.
4. Add the drained rice noodles and Pad Thai sauce to the pan. Toss everything together and cook for 2-3 minutes until the noodles are tender.
5. Stir in the bean sprouts, crushed peanuts, and green onions. Toss for another minute until heated through.
6. Serve hot, optionally garnished with extra peanuts and a lime wedge.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
- Be cautious when stir-frying over high heat to prevent oil splatters.
