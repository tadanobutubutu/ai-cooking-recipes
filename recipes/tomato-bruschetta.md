# Tomato Bruschetta

**⚠️ Allergens**: gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Baguette (sliced): 150 grams
- Ripe tomatoes (diced): 200 grams
- Fresh basil (chopped): 2 tbsp
- Garlic (minced): 1 tsp
- Extra virgin olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Balsamic vinegar: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 0.5 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.25 tsp

## Instructions
1. Toast the baguette slices until golden brown.
2. In a bowl, mix the diced tomatoes, chopped basil, minced garlic, olive oil, balsamic vinegar, salt, and black pepper.
3. Spoon the tomato mixture generously over the toasted baguette slices.
4. Serve immediately.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling hot toast.
