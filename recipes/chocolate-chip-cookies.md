# Chocolate Chip Cookies

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy / gluten / soy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- All-purpose flour: 85 grams
- Unsalted butter (melted): 55 grams
- Brown sugar: 50 grams
- Granulated sugar: 25 grams
- Egg yolk: 15 grams
- Vanilla extract: 0.5 tsp
- Baking soda: 0.25 tsp
- Salt: 0.25 tsp
- Chocolate chips: 50 grams

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the melted butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar until well combined.
3. Whisk in the egg yolk and vanilla extract until smooth.
4. Stir in the all-purpose flour, baking soda, and salt until just combined.
5. Fold in the chocolate chips.
6. Scoop the dough into small balls and place them on the prepared baking sheet.
7. Bake for 10-12 minutes, or until the edges are golden brown.
8. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.

## Safety Notes
- Do not consume raw cookie dough containing unpasteurized eggs or raw flour.
