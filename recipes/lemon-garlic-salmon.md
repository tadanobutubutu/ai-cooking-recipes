# Lemon Garlic Salmon

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Salmon fillets: 400 gram
- Butter: 2 tbsp
- Garlic (minced): 1 tbsp
- Lemon juice: 2 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp
- Parsley (chopped): 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Season the salmon fillets with salt and black pepper.
3. Place salmon in the skillet skin-side down and cook for 5 minutes.
4. Flip the salmon and cook for another 3 minutes.
5. Reduce heat to medium, add butter and minced garlic to the pan.
6. Cook garlic for 1 minute until fragrant.
7. Stir in lemon juice and spoon the sauce over the salmon.
8. Garnish with chopped parsley and serve warm.

## Safety Notes
- Cook salmon to an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C).
