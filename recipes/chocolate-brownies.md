# Chocolate Brownies

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 40 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Unsalted butter: 60g
- Dark chocolate (70% cocoa): 80g
- Granulated sugar: 100g
- Large egg: 1
- Vanilla extract: 1 tsp
- All-purpose flour: 40g
- Cocoa powder: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 0.25 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a small baking dish (e.g., 6x6 inches) with parchment paper.
2. In a heatproof bowl set over a pot of simmering water (double boiler), melt the butter and dark chocolate together until smooth. Remove from heat.
3. Whisk the sugar into the warm chocolate mixture until combined.
4. Whisk in the egg and vanilla extract until the batter becomes smooth and glossy.
5. Fold in the flour, cocoa powder, and salt until just combined. Do not overmix.
6. Pour the batter into the prepared baking dish and smooth the top.
7. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out with moist crumbs.
8. Allow the brownies to cool completely in the pan before lifting them out and slicing.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when using a double boiler to avoid steam burns.
