# Eggs Benedict

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- English Muffins: 120 grams
- Eggs: 200 grams
- Canadian Bacon: 100 grams
- Butter: 115 grams
- Egg Yolks: 50 grams
- Lemon Juice: 1 tbsp
- White Vinegar: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 0.5 tsp
- Black Pepper: 0.25 tsp
- Cayenne Pepper: 0.125 tsp

## Instructions
1. Melt the butter in a small saucepan or microwave until completely liquid but not boiling.
2. In a blender, combine the egg yolks, lemon juice, salt, and cayenne pepper. Blend for 5 seconds until light in color.
3. With the blender running on low, slowly stream in the warm melted butter until the sauce is thick and emulsified. Transfer to a bowl and keep warm.
4. Bring a medium pot of water to a gentle simmer. Add the white vinegar.
5. Crack each egg into a small ramekin or cup. Create a gentle whirlpool in the simmering water and carefully slide the eggs in one by one. Poach for 3 minutes for soft yolks, then remove with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels.
6. While eggs are poaching, toast the English muffin halves and warm the Canadian bacon in a skillet over medium heat for 1-2 minutes per side.
7. To assemble, place two muffin halves on each plate, top each with a slice of Canadian bacon, a poached egg, and a generous spoonful of hollandaise sauce. Garnish with black pepper.

## Safety Notes
- Consume cooked eggs immediately. Raw or undercooked eggs may increase your risk of foodborne illness.
