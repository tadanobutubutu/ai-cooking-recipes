# Spinach Omelette

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Eggs: 200 grams
- Fresh spinach: 60 grams
- Butter: 1 tbsp
- Shredded cheddar cheese: 30 grams
- Salt: 0.5 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.25 tsp

## Instructions
1. Crack the eggs into a bowl, add salt and pepper, and whisk until well combined.
2. Melt the butter in a non-stick skillet over medium heat.
3. Add the fresh spinach to the skillet and sauté until wilted, about 2 minutes.
4. Pour the whisked eggs over the spinach and cook until the edges start to set.
5. Gently lift the edges of the omelette with a spatula, letting the uncooked egg flow underneath.
6. Sprinkle the shredded cheddar cheese over half of the omelette.
7. Fold the other half of the omelette over the cheese and cook for another minute until the cheese is melted and the eggs are fully cooked.
8. Slide the omelette onto a plate, cut in half, and serve immediately.

## Safety Notes
- Wash spinach thoroughly before cooking to remove any dirt.
- Ensure eggs are cooked thoroughly to avoid foodborne illness.
