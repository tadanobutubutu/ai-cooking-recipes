# Mushroom Stroganoff

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten / soy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- button mushrooms (sliced): 300g
- onion (chopped): 100g
- garlic (minced): 2 tsp
- butter: 2 tbsp
- all-purpose flour: 1 tbsp
- vegetable broth: 250g
- sour cream: 100g
- soy sauce: 1 tbsp
- egg noodles: 150g

## Instructions
1. Boil a large pot of salted water and cook the egg noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
2. In a large skillet, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
3. Add the minced garlic and sliced mushrooms. Cook until the mushrooms are browned and have released their moisture, about 8-10 minutes.
4. Sprinkle the flour over the mushroom mixture and stir well to coat. Cook for 1 minute.
5. Gradually pour in the vegetable broth and soy sauce, stirring constantly to prevent lumps. Bring the mixture to a simmer and let it thicken for 3-5 minutes.
6. Remove the skillet from the heat and stir in the sour cream until smooth and well combined.
7. Serve the mushroom stroganoff warm over the cooked egg noodles.

## Safety Notes
- Wash mushrooms thoroughly to remove any dirt or debris before cooking.
