# Tofu Stir Fry

**⚠️ Allergens**: soy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Firm Tofu: 400 grams
- Broccoli: 200 grams
- Carrots: 100 grams
- Soy Sauce: 2 tbsp
- Sesame Oil: 1 tbsp
- Minced Garlic: 2 tsp

## Instructions
1. Press the firm tofu to remove excess water, then cut into 1-inch cubes.
2. Chop the broccoli into florets and slice the carrots thinly.
3. Heat the sesame oil in a large pan or wok over medium-high heat.
4. Add the minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Add the tofu cubes and cook until lightly browned on all sides, about 5-7 minutes.
6. Toss in the broccoli and carrots, cooking for another 5 minutes until tender-crisp.
7. Pour the soy sauce over the mixture and stir well to coat everything evenly.
8. Serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure tofu is pressed properly to avoid oil splattering during stir-frying.
