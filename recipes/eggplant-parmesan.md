# Eggplant Parmesan

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 60 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Eggplant: 400g
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Marinara sauce: 400g
- Mozzarella cheese: 150g
- Parmesan cheese: 50g
- Breadcrumbs: 50g
- Eggs: 50g

## Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Slice the eggplant into 1/2-inch thick rounds.
3. Dip the eggplant slices into the beaten egg, then coat them with breadcrumbs.
4. Place the coated slices on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper and drizzle with olive oil.
5. Bake for 20 minutes, flipping halfway through, until golden brown.
6. In a baking dish, spread a thin layer of marinara sauce.
7. Layer the baked eggplant slices, more marinara sauce, mozzarella cheese, and parmesan cheese. Repeat the layers.
8. Bake for another 20 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
9. Let it rest for 5 minutes before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling the hot baking dish from the oven.
