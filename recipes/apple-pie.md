# Apple Pie

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 60 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Apples (peeled and sliced): 500 grams
- Granulated Sugar: 100 grams
- Ground Cinnamon: 1 tsp
- Lemon Juice: 1 tbsp
- All-Purpose Flour: 1 tbsp
- Pre-made Pie Crust Dough: 250 grams
- Unsalted Butter: 30 grams

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. In a large bowl, mix the sliced apples, granulated sugar, ground cinnamon, lemon juice, and all-purpose flour until the apples are evenly coated.
3. Shape the pre-made pie crust dough into a small pie dish or baking dish suitable for 2 servings.
4. Pour the apple mixture into the pie crust.
5. Cut the unsalted butter into small pieces and dot them evenly over the apple filling.
6. Bake in the preheated oven for 40 to 45 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the apples are tender.
7. Let the pie cool for at least 15 minutes before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling hot dishes from the oven. Let the pie cool slightly before eating to avoid burning your mouth.
