# Crispy Baked Chicken Thighs

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 45 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Bone-in skin-on chicken thighs: 450 grams
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp
- Garlic powder: 1 tsp
- Paprika: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Pat the chicken thighs completely dry with a paper towel. This helps make the skin crispy.
3. In a small bowl, mix the salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and paprika.
4. Rub the chicken thighs with olive oil, then coat them evenly with the spice mixture.
5. Place the chicken thighs on the prepared baking sheet, skin-side up.
6. Bake for 35-40 minutes, or until the skin is crispy and the internal temperature reaches 165°F (75°C).
7. Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
