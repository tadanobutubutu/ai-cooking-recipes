# Mashed Potatoes

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Potatoes (Russet or Yukon Gold): 500g
- Unsalted butter: 4 tbsp
- Milk: 60ml
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into evenly sized chunks.
2. Place the potato chunks in a large pot and cover them with cold water. Add a pinch of salt to the water.
3. Bring the water to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce the heat and simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until the potatoes are fork-tender.
4. Drain the potatoes well and return them to the pot or a large bowl.
5. While the potatoes are hot, add the butter, milk, salt, and black pepper.
6. Mash the potatoes using a potato masher or a ricer until they reach your desired consistency. Be careful not to over-mix, or they may become gummy.
7. Serve hot, garnished with extra butter or fresh chives if desired.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when draining the hot water to avoid burns.
