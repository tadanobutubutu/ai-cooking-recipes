# Black Bean Soup

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Canned black beans, drained and rinsed: 400g
- Vegetable broth: 480g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Onion, diced: 150g
- Garlic, minced: 2 tsp
- Cumin: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a pot over medium heat, then sauté the onion and garlic until softened (about 5 minutes).
2. Add the black beans, vegetable broth, and cumin to the pot.
3. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 15 minutes.
4. Carefully blend a portion of the soup to thicken the texture, then serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Allow hot liquids to cool slightly before blending, or use an immersion blender carefully to avoid splattering.
