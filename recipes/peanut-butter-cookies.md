# Peanut Butter Cookies

**⚠️ Allergens**: nuts / eggs
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Peanut Butter: 250 grams
- Granulated Sugar: 200 grams
- Egg: 50 grams

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. In a medium bowl, mix the peanut butter, granulated sugar, and egg until well combined and smooth.
3. Roll the dough into small balls and place them on the prepared baking sheet.
4. Use a fork to press a crisscross pattern into the top of each cookie.
5. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes, or until the edges are golden brown.
6. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.

## Safety Notes
- Wash hands thoroughly after handling raw egg.
- Use oven mitts when handling hot baking sheets.
