# Creamy Tuscan Chicken

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken Breasts: 400g
- Olive Oil: 1 tbsp
- Butter: 1 tbsp
- Garlic: 2 cloves, minced
- Cherry Tomatoes: 150g, halved
- Spinach: 100g
- Heavy Cream: 120ml
- Parmesan Cheese: 30g, grated

## Instructions
1. Season chicken breasts with salt and pepper.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken and cook for 5-7 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Remove and set aside.
3. In the same skillet, melt butter over medium heat. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Add cherry tomatoes and cook until they start to burst, about 3 minutes.
5. Stir in spinach and cook until wilted.
6. Pour in heavy cream and bring to a gentle simmer. Stir in parmesan cheese until melted and the sauce thickens slightly.
7. Return chicken to the skillet, coat with the sauce, and simmer for 2 more minutes. Serve warm.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
