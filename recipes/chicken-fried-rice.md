# Chicken Fried Rice

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / soy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Cooked jasmine rice (day-old): 300g
- Boneless, skinless chicken breast: 150g
- Eggs: 2
- Frozen peas and carrots: 50g
- Soy sauce: 2 tbsp
- Sesame oil: 1 tbsp
- Vegetable oil: 1 tbsp
- Garlic, minced: 1 tsp
- Green onions, chopped: 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1/4 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. Cut the chicken breast into bite-sized pieces and season with salt and pepper.
2. Heat the vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook until browned and cooked through, about 5 minutes.
3. Push the chicken to one side of the wok and add the minced garlic, cooking for 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. Add the frozen peas and carrots, stirring for 2 minutes until heated through.
5. Push the vegetables and chicken to the side and crack the eggs into the empty space. Scramble them until fully cooked.
6. Add the day-old rice to the wok, breaking up any clumps. Stir everything together.
7. Pour the soy sauce and sesame oil over the rice. Stir-fry for 3-4 minutes, ensuring all ingredients are well combined and heated through.
8. Remove from heat, garnish with chopped green onions, and serve immediately.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
