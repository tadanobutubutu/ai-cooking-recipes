# Turkey Chili

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 40 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Ground turkey: 450 grams
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Onion, chopped: 150 grams
- Bell pepper, chopped: 150 grams
- Garlic, minced: 1 tbsp
- Chili powder: 2 tbsp
- Cumin: 1 tbsp
- Diced tomatoes (canned): 400 grams
- Kidney beans (canned, drained): 400 grams
- Chicken broth: 240 grams
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Add chopped onion, bell pepper, and minced garlic. Sauté until softened, about 5 minutes.
3. Add ground turkey to the pot, breaking it apart with a spoon. Cook until browned, about 8 minutes.
4. Stir in chili powder, cumin, salt, and black pepper. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Pour in the diced tomatoes, kidney beans, and chicken broth. Stir well to combine.
6. Bring the chili to a boil, then reduce the heat to low. Cover and simmer for 25 minutes, stirring occasionally.
7. Remove from heat and serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Cook ground turkey to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
