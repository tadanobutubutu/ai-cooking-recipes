# Mushroom Omelette

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Eggs (beaten): 200 grams
- White mushrooms (sliced): 100 grams
- Butter: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 0.5 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.25 tsp
- Cheddar cheese (grated): 50 grams

## Instructions
1. Melt half of the butter in a non-stick skillet over medium heat.
2. Add the sliced mushrooms to the skillet and sauté for 5 minutes until browned and tender. Remove from the skillet and set aside.
3. Melt the remaining butter in the skillet over medium-low heat.
4. Pour in the beaten eggs and cook for 2-3 minutes, gently pushing the edges towards the center to allow the uncooked egg to flow underneath.
5. When the eggs are mostly set but still slightly runny on top, sprinkle the salt, black pepper, and cheddar cheese over one half of the omelette.
6. Top the cheese with the sautéed mushrooms.
7. Gently fold the other half of the omelette over the filling.
8. Cook for another 1 minute until the cheese melts, then slide the omelette onto a plate and cut in half to serve.

## Safety Notes
- Handle hot pans and butter carefully to avoid burns.
- Ensure eggs are cooked to your desired level of doneness.
