# Tuna Melt

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Canned tuna (drained): 150g
- Mayonnaise: 2 tbsp
- Celery (finely chopped): 20g
- Red onion (finely diced): 15g
- Salt: 0.5 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.25 tsp
- Sliced bread: 4 slices
- Cheddar cheese: 4 slices
- Butter (softened): 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat a skillet over medium-low heat.
2. In a medium bowl, combine the drained tuna, mayonnaise, celery, red onion, salt, and black pepper. Mix well.
3. Lay out the 4 slices of bread. Divide the tuna mixture evenly onto 2 of the slices.
4. Top the tuna mixture on each slice with 2 slices of cheddar cheese.
5. Place the remaining 2 slices of bread on top to form sandwiches.
6. Spread the softened butter evenly on the outside of both sandwiches.
7. Place the sandwiches in the preheated skillet and cook for 3-4 minutes on each side, or until the bread is golden brown and the cheese is melted.
8. Remove from the skillet, let cool for a minute, slice in half, and serve.

## Safety Notes
- Handle the skillet carefully to avoid burns.
- Ensure the canned tuna is fully drained to prevent a soggy sandwich.
