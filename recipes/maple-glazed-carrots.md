# Maple Glazed Carrots

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Carrots (peeled and sliced): 300 grams
- Maple syrup: 2 tbsp
- Butter: 1 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. In a bowl, toss the sliced carrots with olive oil, salt, and black pepper.
3. Spread the carrots evenly on a baking sheet.
4. Roast in the preheated oven for 20 minutes, or until tender.
5. In a small microwave-safe bowl, melt the butter and mix with maple syrup.
6. Remove carrots from the oven, pour the maple-butter mixture over them, and toss to coat evenly.
7. Return to the oven and roast for an additional 5 minutes until caramelized.

## Safety Notes
- Always use oven mitts when handling hot baking sheets.
- Cut carrots evenly to ensure uniform cooking.
