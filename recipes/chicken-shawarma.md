# Chicken Shawarma

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken breast: 400 grams
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Lemon juice: 1 tbsp
- Garlic powder: 1 tsp
- Ground cumin: 1 tsp
- Ground coriander: 1 tsp
- Paprika: 1 tsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Slice the chicken breast into thin strips.
3. In a bowl, mix olive oil, lemon juice, garlic powder, ground cumin, ground coriander, paprika, salt, and black pepper.
4. Toss the chicken strips in the marinade until evenly coated.
5. Spread the chicken strips on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
6. Bake for 25 minutes, or until the chicken is fully cooked and slightly charred at the edges.
7. Remove from oven and let it rest for 5 minutes before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
- Wash hands and all surfaces after handling raw chicken.
