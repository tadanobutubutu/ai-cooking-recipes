# Pesto Pasta

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten / nuts
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Dried Pasta (Penne or Spaghetti): 200 grams
- Fresh Basil Leaves: 50 grams
- Pine Nuts: 3 tbsp
- Parmesan Cheese (Grated): 50 grams
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Extra Virgin Olive Oil: 100 ml
- Salt: 1/2 tsp
- Black Pepper: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the pasta and cook according to package instructions until al dente.
2. While the pasta cooks, lightly toast the pine nuts in a dry pan over medium heat for 2-3 minutes until golden. Let them cool.
3. In a food processor, combine the fresh basil, toasted pine nuts, grated Parmesan cheese, garlic cloves, salt, and black pepper. Pulse until coarsely chopped.
4. With the food processor running, slowly drizzle in the extra virgin olive oil until the mixture becomes a smooth paste.
5. Reserve 1/4 cup of the pasta cooking water, then drain the pasta.
6. Return the pasta to the pot (off the heat). Add the pesto sauce and toss well to coat. Add a splash of the reserved pasta water if needed to loosen the sauce.
7. Serve immediately, garnished with extra Parmesan cheese and fresh basil leaves if desired.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when draining the hot pasta water.
- Ensure the food processor is completely stopped before scraping down the sides.
