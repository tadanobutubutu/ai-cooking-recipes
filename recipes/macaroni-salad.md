# Macaroni Salad

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Dry macaroni pasta: 115g
- Mayonnaise: 4 tbsp
- Yellow mustard: 1 tsp
- White vinegar: 1 tbsp
- Sugar: 1 tbsp
- Celery, finely chopped: 30g
- Red onion, finely diced: 20g
- Hard-boiled egg, chopped: 1
- Salt: 1/2 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil. Add the macaroni pasta and cook for 8-10 minutes until tender.
2. Drain the pasta and rinse under cold water to cool it down.
3. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, yellow mustard, white vinegar, sugar, salt, and black pepper.
4. Add the cooled macaroni, chopped celery, diced red onion, and chopped hard-boiled egg to the bowl.
5. Gently toss everything together until the pasta is well coated with the dressing.
6. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving to allow the flavors to meld.

## Safety Notes
- Keep the salad refrigerated and do not leave it at room temperature for more than 2 hours to prevent bacterial growth.
