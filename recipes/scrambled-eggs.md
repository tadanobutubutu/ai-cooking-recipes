# Scrambled Eggs

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 10 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Eggs: 200 gram
- Milk: 2 tbsp
- Butter: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Crack the eggs into a bowl.
2. Add milk, salt, and black pepper to the bowl and whisk until smooth.
3. Melt butter in a non-stick skillet over medium-low heat.
4. Pour the egg mixture into the skillet.
5. Gently stir the eggs with a spatula as they cook, pushing them from the edges to the center.
6. Remove from heat when the eggs are mostly set but still slightly soft and shiny. They will continue to cook off the heat.

## Safety Notes
- Wash hands thoroughly after handling raw eggs to avoid salmonella risk.
