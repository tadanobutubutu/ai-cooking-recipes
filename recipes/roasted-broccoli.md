# Roasted Broccoli

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Broccoli florets: 300 grams
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp
- Garlic powder: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Wash and dry the broccoli florets thoroughly.
3. In a bowl, toss the broccoli florets with olive oil, salt, black pepper, and garlic powder until well coated.
4. Spread the broccoli evenly on a baking sheet in a single layer.
5. Roast in the preheated oven for 15-20 minutes, or until the edges are crispy and browned.
6. Remove from the oven and serve immediately.

## Safety Notes
- Use oven mitts when handling hot baking sheets.
- Ensure the broccoli is completely dry before tossing with oil to prevent steaming instead of roasting.
