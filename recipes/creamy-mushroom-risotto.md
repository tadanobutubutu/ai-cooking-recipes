# Creamy Mushroom Risotto

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 40 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Arborio Rice: 150g
- Vegetable Broth: 500g
- Mushrooms (cremini or button): 200g
- Onion (finely chopped): 50g
- Butter: 30g
- Parmesan Cheese (grated): 30g

## Instructions
1. In a saucepan, warm the vegetable broth over low heat.
2. In a separate large pan, melt half the butter and sauté the chopped onion until translucent.
3. Add the mushrooms and cook until browned.
4. Stir in the Arborio rice and toast for 1-2 minutes.
5. Gradually add the warm broth, one ladle at a time, stirring constantly and allowing the liquid to absorb before adding more.
6. Continue this process until the rice is creamy and cooked al dente (about 20 minutes).
7. Remove from heat and stir in the remaining butter and Parmesan cheese.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure mushrooms are thoroughly cleaned before cooking.
