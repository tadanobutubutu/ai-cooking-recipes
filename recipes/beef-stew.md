# Beef Stew

**⚠️ Allergens**: gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 120 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Beef chuck roast, cubed: 400g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Onion, chopped: 100g
- Carrots, peeled and chopped: 150g
- Potatoes, peeled and cubed: 200g
- Garlic, minced: 1 tbsp
- All-purpose flour: 2 tbsp
- Beef broth: 400g
- Tomato paste: 1 tbsp
- Dried thyme: 1 tsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add beef cubes and brown on all sides.
2. Add onion, carrots, and garlic to the pot. Cook until onions are softened, about 5 minutes.
3. Sprinkle flour over the beef and vegetables, stirring well to coat. Cook for 1 minute.
4. Stir in beef broth, tomato paste, thyme, salt, and black pepper. Bring to a simmer.
5. Add the potatoes. Cover the pot, reduce heat to low, and simmer for 90 minutes, or until the beef is tender.
6. Serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Cook beef to an internal temperature of at least 145°F (63°C).
- Handle hot liquids carefully to avoid burns.
