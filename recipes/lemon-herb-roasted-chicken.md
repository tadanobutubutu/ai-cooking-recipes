# Lemon Herb Roasted Chicken

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 45 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken thighs (bone-in, skin-on): 400 grams
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Lemon juice: 2 tbsp
- Lemon zest: 1 tsp
- Minced garlic: 2 tsp
- Dried rosemary: 1 tsp
- Dried thyme: 1 tsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, minced garlic, dried rosemary, dried thyme, salt, and black pepper.
3. Pat the chicken thighs dry with paper towels and place them in a baking dish.
4. Pour the lemon herb mixture over the chicken, making sure to coat the pieces evenly.
5. Roast in the preheated oven for 35 to 40 minutes, or until the chicken is golden brown and cooked through.
6. Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
- Wash hands and surfaces thoroughly after handling raw chicken.
