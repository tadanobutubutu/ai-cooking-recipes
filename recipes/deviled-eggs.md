# Deviled Eggs

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Eggs: 4 large (about 200g)
- Mayonnaise: 2 tbsp
- Dijon mustard: 1 tsp
- White vinegar: 1 tsp
- Salt: 1/4 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp
- Paprika: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. Place eggs in a single layer in a saucepan and cover with water by 1 inch.
2. Bring to a rolling boil over high heat.
3. Remove from heat, cover, and let stand for 12 minutes.
4. Drain and immediately transfer eggs to a bowl of ice water to cool completely.
5. Peel the eggs and slice them in half lengthwise.
6. Gently remove the yolks and place them in a medium bowl. Set the egg whites aside on a serving platter.
7. Mash the yolks with a fork until very smooth.
8. Stir in mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, white vinegar, salt, and black pepper until well combined and creamy.
9. Spoon or pipe the yolk mixture evenly into the egg white halves.
10. Sprinkle lightly with paprika before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure eggs are cooked thoroughly and store prepared deviled eggs in the refrigerator to prevent bacterial growth.
