# Banana Bread

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 60 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Ripe bananas, mashed: 225g
- Melted butter: 75g
- Baking soda: 1 tsp
- Salt: 0.25 tsp
- Sugar: 100g
- Egg, beaten: 1 large
- Vanilla extract: 1 tsp
- All-purpose flour: 190g

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 4x8 inch loaf pan.
2. In a mixing bowl, mash the ripe bananas with a fork until completely smooth.
3. Stir the melted butter into the mashed bananas.
4. Mix in the baking soda and salt.
5. Stir in the sugar, beaten egg, and vanilla extract.
6. Mix in the flour until just incorporated.
7. Pour the batter into your prepared loaf pan.
8. Bake for 50-60 minutes, or until a tester inserted into the center comes out clean.
9. Remove from oven and let cool in the pan for a few minutes. Then, remove the banana bread from the pan and let cool completely before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Always wash hands after handling raw eggs.
- Use oven mitts when placing the loaf pan in and removing it from the oven to prevent burns.
