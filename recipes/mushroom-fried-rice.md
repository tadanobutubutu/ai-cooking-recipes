# Mushroom Fried Rice

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / gluten / soy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Cooked white rice: 400 grams
- Button mushrooms (sliced): 200 grams
- Soy sauce: 2 tbsp
- Eggs: 100 grams
- Green onions (chopped): 20 grams
- Vegetable oil: 1 tbsp
- Garlic (minced): 10 grams

## Instructions
1. Heat vegetable oil in a large pan over medium-high heat.
2. Add minced garlic and sliced mushrooms, sautéing until mushrooms are tender.
3. Push the mushrooms to one side of the pan, pour the beaten eggs onto the other side, and scramble.
4. Add the cooked white rice to the pan and mix everything together.
5. Drizzle soy sauce over the mixture and stir well to ensure the rice is evenly coated.
6. Stir in the chopped green onions and cook for another minute.
7. Serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure cooked rice is stored and reheated properly to prevent foodborne illness.
