# Banana Pancakes

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Ripe bananas: 200g
- All-purpose flour: 125g
- Milk: 120g
- Large egg: 1 (approx. 50g)
- Baking powder: 2 tsp
- Vanilla extract: 1 tsp
- Butter: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 0.25 tsp

## Instructions
1. In a medium bowl, mash the ripe bananas with a fork until smooth.
2. Crack the egg into the mashed bananas, add the milk and vanilla extract, and whisk until well combined.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the all-purpose flour, baking powder, and salt.
4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and gently stir until just combined (do not overmix; a few lumps are fine).
5. Heat a large skillet or griddle over medium heat and melt a small amount of the butter.
6. Pour 1/4 cup portions of the batter onto the skillet for each pancake.
7. Cook for 2-3 minutes, or until bubbles appear on the surface and the edges look set.
8. Flip the pancakes and cook for another 1-2 minutes until golden brown and cooked through.
9. Serve warm with your favorite toppings.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when flipping pancakes to avoid splattering hot butter.
- Ensure the skillet is not too hot to prevent the outside of the pancakes from burning before the inside is cooked.
