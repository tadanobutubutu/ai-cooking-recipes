# Turkey Meatballs

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Ground turkey: 250 grams
- Breadcrumbs: 30 grams
- Parmesan cheese, grated: 20 grams
- Egg: 1
- Garlic powder: 1 tsp
- Onion powder: 1 tsp
- Dried oregano: 1 tsp
- Salt: 1/2 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. In a medium bowl, combine ground turkey, breadcrumbs, Parmesan cheese, egg, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, salt, and pepper. Mix until just combined.
3. Form the mixture into 1-inch meatballs and place them on the prepared baking sheet.
4. Bake for 15-20 minutes, or until the meatballs are cooked through and golden brown on the outside.

## Safety Notes
- Cook ground turkey to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
