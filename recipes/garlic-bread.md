# Garlic Bread

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Baguette: 150g
- Butter (softened): 3 tbsp
- Garlic (minced): 2 cloves
- Fresh Parsley (chopped): 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Slice the baguette horizontally into two halves.
3. In a small bowl, mix the softened butter, minced garlic, and chopped parsley.
4. Spread the garlic butter mixture evenly over the cut sides of the baguette.
5. Place the baguette halves on a baking sheet and bake for 10-12 minutes, or until the edges are golden brown and crispy.
6. Remove from the oven, let it cool slightly, slice, and serve.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when using the oven and handling hot baking sheets.
