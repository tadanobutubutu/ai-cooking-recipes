# Honey Garlic Pork Chops

**⚠️ Allergens**: soy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Pork chops: 400 grams
- Soy sauce: 2 tbsp
- Honey: 2 tbsp
- Minced garlic: 1 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Season the pork chops with salt and black pepper on both sides.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Add the pork chops and sear for 4 to 5 minutes on each side until golden brown.
4. Reduce the heat to medium. Add the minced garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Stir in the soy sauce and honey. Let the sauce simmer for 2 to 3 minutes until it thickens and coats the pork chops.
6. Remove from heat and serve immediately with your favorite side dishes.

## Safety Notes
- Cook pork chops to an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C), followed by a 3-minute rest.
