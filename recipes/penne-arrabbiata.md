# Penne Arrabbiata

**⚠️ Allergens**: gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Penne pasta: 200 grams
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Minced garlic: 15 grams
- Red pepper flakes: 1 tsp
- Canned crushed tomatoes: 400 grams
- Fresh parsley, chopped: 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Boil a large pot of salted water and cook the penne pasta according to package instructions until al dente. Reserve a bit of pasta water before draining.
2. In a large pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and red pepper flakes, cooking for about 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Pour the crushed tomatoes into the pan, add salt and black pepper, and stir well. Let the sauce simmer for 10-15 minutes until it thickens slightly.
4. Toss the cooked penne into the sauce, mixing thoroughly to coat the pasta. Add a splash of reserved pasta water if needed.
5. Garnish with chopped fresh parsley and serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when cooking with chili flakes, avoid touching your eyes.
- Ensure the sauce doesn't splatter by keeping the heat at a low simmer once it starts bubbling.
