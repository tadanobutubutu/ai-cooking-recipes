# Fruit Salad

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Apple: 150g
- Banana: 120g
- Grapes: 100g
- Strawberries: 100g
- Honey: 1 tbsp
- Lemon juice: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Wash all the fruits thoroughly under cold water.
2. Peel and chop the apple and banana into bite-sized pieces.
3. Halve the grapes and strawberries.
4. In a large bowl, combine the chopped apple, banana, grapes, and strawberries.
5. In a small bowl, whisk together the honey and lemon juice.
6. Pour the honey-lemon dressing over the fruit and gently toss to combine.
7. Serve immediately or chill in the refrigerator before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when using a knife to chop the fruits. Wash produce thoroughly to remove pesticides or dirt.
