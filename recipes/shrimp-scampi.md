# Shrimp Scampi

**⚠️ Allergens**: shellfish / dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Large shrimp, peeled and deveined: 340g
- Unsalted butter: 2 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Garlic, minced: 1 tbsp
- Lemon juice: 2 tbsp
- Fresh parsley, chopped: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp
- Dried red pepper flakes: 1/2 tsp

## Instructions
1. In a large skillet, melt the butter and olive oil over medium heat.
2. Add the minced garlic and red pepper flakes, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add the shrimp to the skillet and cook for 2 minutes per side until they turn pink.
4. Stir in the lemon juice, salt, and black pepper, simmering for 1 additional minute.
5. Remove the skillet from heat and garnish the shrimp with fresh parsley before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure shrimp are cooked to an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C).
