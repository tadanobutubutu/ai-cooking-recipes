# Fish Tacos

**⚠️ Allergens**: gluten / dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- White fish fillets: 400 grams
- Flour tortillas: 4
- Cabbage slaw: 100 grams
- Sour cream: 2 tbsp
- Taco seasoning: 1 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Lime: 1

## Instructions
1. Season the fish fillets evenly with the taco seasoning.
2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Cook the fish for 3-4 minutes per side until opaque and easily flaked with a fork.
4. Warm the flour tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave.
5. Flake the cooked fish and divide it among the warmed tortillas.
6. Top with cabbage slaw and sour cream, then serve with a squeeze of fresh lime juice.

## Safety Notes
- Cook fish to an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C).
