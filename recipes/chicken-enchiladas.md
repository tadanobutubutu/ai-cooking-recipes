# Chicken Enchiladas

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 45 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken breast (cooked and shredded): 300g
- Enchilada sauce: 300g
- Flour tortillas: 4
- Cheddar cheese (shredded): 150g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Onion (diced): 100g

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat, add the diced onion, and sauté until softened.
3. Mix the shredded chicken, sautéed onion, and half of the enchilada sauce in a bowl.
4. Spoon the chicken mixture into each tortilla, roll them up, and place them seam-side down in a baking dish.
5. Pour the remaining enchilada sauce over the rolled tortillas and sprinkle evenly with shredded cheddar cheese.
6. Bake for 20-25 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
7. Let cool for a few minutes before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure chicken is cooked to a minimum internal temperature of 165°F (75°C) before shredding.
