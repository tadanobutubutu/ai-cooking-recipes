# French Bread Pizza

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- French bread: 1 loaf (about 250g)
- Pizza sauce: 120g
- Mozzarella cheese (shredded): 150g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Garlic powder: 1 tsp
- Dried oregano: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Cut the French bread in half lengthwise to create two pizza bases.
3. Brush the cut sides of the bread with olive oil and sprinkle with garlic powder.
4. Spread the pizza sauce evenly over each half of the bread.
5. Sprinkle the shredded mozzarella cheese generously over the sauce.
6. Top with dried oregano.
7. Place the bread on a baking sheet and bake for 10-12 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
8. Let cool for a few minutes before slicing and serving.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when removing the baking sheet from the oven, as it will be hot.
- Use caution when slicing the pizza to avoid burns from the hot cheese.
