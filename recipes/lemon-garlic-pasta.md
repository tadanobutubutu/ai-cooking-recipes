# Lemon Garlic Pasta

**⚠️ Allergens**: gluten / dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Spaghetti: 200g
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Butter: 2 tbsp
- Garlic, minced: 3 cloves
- Red pepper flakes: 1/2 tsp
- Lemon juice: 2 tbsp
- Lemon zest: 1 tsp
- Parmesan cheese, grated: 50g
- Fresh parsley, chopped: 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp

## Instructions
1. Boil a large pot of salted water. Add the spaghetti and cook until al dente according to package instructions. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water, then drain the pasta.
2. While the pasta cooks, heat olive oil and butter in a large skillet over medium heat until the butter melts.
3. Add the minced garlic and red pepper flakes to the skillet. Sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant, being careful not to burn the garlic.
4. Stir in the lemon juice and lemon zest.
5. Add the cooked pasta to the skillet, tossing to coat the noodles in the sauce. If the pasta seems dry, add a splash of the reserved pasta water.
6. Remove from heat and stir in the grated Parmesan cheese and chopped parsley. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
7. Serve immediately, garnished with extra Parmesan and parsley if desired.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful not to burn the garlic, as it can turn bitter quickly.
