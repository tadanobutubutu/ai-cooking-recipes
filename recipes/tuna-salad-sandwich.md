# Tuna Salad Sandwich

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 10 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Canned tuna (drained): 150g
- Mayonnaise: 2 tbsp
- Celery (finely chopped): 30g
- Red onion (finely chopped): 15g
- Lemon juice: 1 tsp
- Sliced bread: 4 slices

## Instructions
1. In a medium bowl, combine the drained tuna, mayonnaise, celery, red onion, and lemon juice.
2. Mix well until all ingredients are evenly incorporated.
3. Toast the bread slices if desired.
4. Divide the tuna salad mixture evenly between two slices of bread.
5. Top with the remaining two slices of bread to form sandwiches.

## Safety Notes
- Store any leftover tuna salad in an airtight container in the refrigerator and consume within 2 days.
