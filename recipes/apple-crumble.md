# Apple Crumble

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 45 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Apples (Granny Smith): 300g
- Granulated sugar: 50g
- Ground cinnamon: 1 tsp
- Lemon juice: 1 tbsp
- All-purpose flour: 100g
- Unsalted butter (cold, cubed): 50g
- Brown sugar: 50g
- Rolled oats: 30g

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Peel, core, and slice the apples into bite-sized pieces.
3. In a bowl, toss the apple slices with granulated sugar, cinnamon, and lemon juice. Transfer the mixture to a small baking dish.
4. In a separate bowl, combine the all-purpose flour, cold cubed butter, brown sugar, and rolled oats.
5. Use your fingers to rub the butter into the dry ingredients until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
6. Sprinkle the crumble mixture evenly over the apples in the baking dish.
7. Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until the topping is golden brown and the apple filling is bubbly.
8. Let it cool for 10 minutes before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when removing the baking dish from the oven as it will be very hot.
