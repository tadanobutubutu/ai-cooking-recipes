# Pork Fried Rice

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / soy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- cooked white rice (preferably day-old): 400 grams
- pork loin (diced): 200 grams
- eggs (beaten): 2
- soy sauce: 2 tbsp
- vegetable oil: 2 tbsp
- frozen mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn): 100 grams
- green onions (chopped): 2 tbsp
- sesame oil: 1 tsp
- salt: 1 tsp
- black pepper: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Heat 1 tbsp of vegetable oil in a large pan or wok over medium-high heat.
2. Add the diced pork loin, salt, and black pepper. Cook until the pork is browned and cooked through, about 5-7 minutes. Remove pork from the pan and set aside.
3. In the same pan, add the remaining 1 tbsp of vegetable oil. Pour in the beaten eggs and scramble until fully cooked.
4. Add the frozen mixed vegetables to the pan and cook for 2 minutes until heated through.
5. Add the cooked white rice and the cooked pork back into the pan. Stir well to combine, breaking up any clumps of rice.
6. Pour the soy sauce and sesame oil over the rice. Stir-fry for 3-5 minutes, ensuring everything is evenly coated and heated.
7. Garnish with chopped green onions before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook pork to an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C) and let it rest for 3 minutes before consuming.
- Wash hands and surfaces after handling raw pork and eggs.
