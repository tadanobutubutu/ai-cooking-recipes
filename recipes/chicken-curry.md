# Chicken Curry

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Boneless, skinless chicken breast: 300g
- Yellow onion: 150g
- Garlic: 10g
- Fresh ginger: 10g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Curry powder: 2 tbsp
- Tomato paste: 2 tbsp
- Chicken broth: 200ml
- Heavy cream: 50ml
- Salt: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Dice the chicken breast into bite-sized pieces. Finely chop the yellow onion, mince the garlic, and grate the fresh ginger.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet or pot over medium heat.
3. Add the chopped onion to the skillet and sauté for 5 minutes until softened and translucent.
4. Stir in the minced garlic and grated ginger, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Add the curry powder to the skillet and cook for 1 minute, stirring constantly.
6. Add the diced chicken breast, cooking for 5-7 minutes until browned on all sides.
7. Stir in the tomato paste, chicken broth, and salt. Bring to a gentle simmer.
8. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and let it simmer for 15 minutes until the chicken is tender and cooked through.
9. Stir in the heavy cream and simmer for 2 more minutes. Remove from heat and serve.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
