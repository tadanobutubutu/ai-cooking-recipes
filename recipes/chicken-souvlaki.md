# Chicken Souvlaki

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken breast: 400 grams
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Lemon juice: 2 tbsp
- Dried oregano: 1 tsp
- Garlic powder: 1 tsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Cut the chicken breast into bite-sized pieces.
2. In a bowl, mix olive oil, lemon juice, dried oregano, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper.
3. Add the chicken pieces to the marinade and let it sit for at least 15 minutes.
4. Thread the marinated chicken pieces onto skewers.
5. Heat a grill pan or skillet over medium-high heat.
6. Cook the chicken skewers for 10 to 12 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden brown and cooked through.
7. Remove from heat and serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
