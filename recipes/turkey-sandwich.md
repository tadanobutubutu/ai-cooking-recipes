# Turkey Sandwich

**⚠️ Allergens**: gluten / dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 5 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Sliced turkey breast: 200 grams
- Whole wheat bread: 4 slices
- Cheddar cheese: 2 slices
- Mayonnaise: 2 tbsp
- Lettuce leaves: 4 leaves
- Tomato, sliced: 1 medium

## Instructions
1. Lay out the 4 slices of bread on a clean surface.
2. Spread 1/2 tablespoon of mayonnaise on one side of each slice.
3. Layer the sliced turkey breast evenly on two of the bread slices.
4. Top the turkey with a slice of cheddar cheese, lettuce leaves, and tomato slices.
5. Place the remaining bread slices on top, mayonnaise side down.
6. Cut the sandwiches in half and serve immediately.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure deli turkey is stored properly in the refrigerator before use.
