# Lentil Soup

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 40 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Green or brown lentils: 100g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Onion, chopped: 75g
- Carrot, diced: 50g
- Celery, diced: 50g
- Garlic, minced: 2 cloves
- Vegetable broth: 480ml
- Diced tomatoes (canned): 200g
- Cumin: 1 tsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Rinse the lentils under cold water and set aside.
2. Heat the olive oil in a pot over medium heat.
3. Add the chopped onion, carrot, and celery. Cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
4. Add the minced garlic and cumin. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Stir in the lentils, diced tomatoes, and vegetable broth.
6. Bring the soup to a boil, then reduce the heat to a simmer.
7. Cover and cook for 25-30 minutes, or until the lentils are tender.
8. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
9. Serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling hot liquids to avoid burns.
