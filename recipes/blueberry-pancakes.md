# Blueberry Pancakes

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- All-purpose flour: 150g
- Sugar: 2 tbsp
- Baking powder: 2 tsp
- Salt: 0.5 tsp
- Milk: 240g
- Egg: 1 large
- Unsalted butter (melted): 2 tbsp
- Fresh blueberries: 100g

## Instructions
1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
2. In another bowl, beat the egg, then whisk in the milk and melted butter.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined. Fold in the blueberries.
4. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat.
5. Pour or scoop the batter onto the griddle, using approximately 1/4 cup for each pancake. Brown on both sides and serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when working with a hot stove or griddle to avoid burns.
