# Baked Apples

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 45 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Apples (Fuji or Gala): 300 grams
- Brown sugar: 2 tbsp
- Ground cinnamon: 1 tsp
- Unsalted butter: 2 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Wash the apples and core them, leaving the bottom intact.
3. In a small bowl, mix the brown sugar and ground cinnamon.
4. Spoon the sugar mixture equally into the center of each apple.
5. Top each apple with 1 tbsp of unsalted butter.
6. Place the apples in a small baking dish and pour a little water into the bottom of the dish to prevent burning.
7. Bake for 35-45 minutes until the apples are tender but not mushy.
8. Let the apples cool slightly before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling the hot baking dish and hot apples to avoid burns.
