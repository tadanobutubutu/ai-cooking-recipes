# Chicken Tacos

**⚠️ Allergens**: gluten / dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken breast: 300g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Taco seasoning: 2 tbsp
- Flour tortillas: 4 small (150g)
- Cheddar cheese (shredded): 50g
- Lettuce (shredded): 50g
- Salsa: 4 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Dice the chicken breast into small bite-sized pieces.
2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Add the diced chicken and taco seasoning to the skillet, stirring until evenly coated.
4. Cook the chicken for 7-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until fully cooked through.
5. Warm the flour tortillas in a separate dry skillet or microwave for 30 seconds.
6. Assemble the tacos by dividing the cooked chicken evenly among the tortillas.
7. Top with shredded lettuce, cheddar cheese, and salsa.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
