# Vegetable Fried Rice

**⚠️ Allergens**: soy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- White Rice (cooked): 400g
- Carrots: 100g
- Peas: 100g
- Onion: 50g
- Soy Sauce: 2 tbsp
- Sesame Oil: 1 tbsp
- Vegetable Oil: 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Dice the carrots and onion.
2. Heat vegetable oil in a wok or large pan over medium-high heat.
3. Add the diced carrots and onions to the pan and stir-fry for 3-4 minutes.
4. Add the peas and cook for another 2 minutes.
5. Add the cooked white rice to the pan and stir well to combine.
6. Pour the soy sauce and sesame oil over the rice and vegetables.
7. Stir-fry for another 3-5 minutes until everything is heated through and well mixed.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when cooking with hot oil to avoid splattering.
