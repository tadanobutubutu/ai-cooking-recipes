# Breakfast Burrito

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Large flour tortillas: 2
- Eggs: 4
- Cheddar cheese (shredded): 50g
- Breakfast sausage (cooked and crumbled): 100g
- Butter: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1/4 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. In a bowl, whisk the eggs, salt, and black pepper until well combined.
2. Melt the butter in a non-stick skillet over medium heat.
3. Pour the egg mixture into the skillet and cook, stirring gently, until scrambled and just set.
4. Warm the flour tortillas slightly to make them pliable.
5. Divide the scrambled eggs, cooked sausage, and shredded cheddar cheese evenly between the two tortillas.
6. Fold the sides of each tortilla inward, then roll them up tightly from the bottom to form burritos.
7. Optional: Toast the burritos in a dry skillet over medium heat for 1-2 minutes per side until golden brown and the cheese is melted.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure breakfast sausage is cooked to an internal temperature of 160°F (71°C) before adding it to the burrito.
