# Garlic Butter Scallops

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / shellfish
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Scallops: 300 gram
- Butter: 2 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Garlic (minced): 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp
- Lemon juice: 1 tbsp
- Parsley (chopped): 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Pat the scallops dry with a paper towel.
2. Season both sides of the scallops with salt and black pepper.
3. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
4. Add the scallops to the skillet in a single layer and sear for 2 minutes without moving them.
5. Flip the scallops, add the butter and minced garlic to the skillet.
6. Cook for another 2 minutes, basting the scallops with the melted garlic butter.
7. Remove from heat, drizzle with lemon juice, and garnish with chopped parsley.

## Safety Notes
- Cook scallops until they reach an internal temperature of 130°F (54°C) to prevent foodborne illness.
