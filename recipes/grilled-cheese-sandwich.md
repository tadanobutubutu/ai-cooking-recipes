# Grilled Cheese Sandwich

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 10 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- White Bread: 4 slices
- Cheddar Cheese: 4 slices
- Butter: 2 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Heat a skillet over medium-low heat.
2. Butter one side of each bread slice.
3. Place two slices of bread, butter-side down, in the skillet.
4. Top each slice with two slices of cheddar cheese.
5. Place the remaining two slices of bread on top, butter-side up.
6. Cook for 3-4 minutes until the bottom is golden brown.
7. Flip the sandwiches and cook for another 3-4 minutes until the cheese is melted and the bread is golden brown.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when flipping the sandwiches to avoid hot butter splattering.
