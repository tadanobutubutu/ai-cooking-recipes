# Lemon Garlic Chicken

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken breast: 400 grams
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Garlic (minced): 1 tbsp
- Lemon juice: 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Season the chicken breasts evenly with salt and black pepper.
3. Add the chicken to the skillet and cook for 6 to 8 minutes on each side until golden brown.
4. Stir in the minced garlic and lemon juice, cooking for an additional 1 to 2 minutes until fragrant.
5. Remove from heat and serve immediately.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
