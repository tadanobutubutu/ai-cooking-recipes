# Vegetable Lasagna

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 55 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Lasagna noodles: 150g
- Ricotta cheese: 200g
- Mozzarella cheese (shredded): 150g
- Parmesan cheese (grated): 50g
- Tomato sauce: 400g
- Zucchini (diced): 150g
- Bell pepper (diced): 100g
- Spinach (chopped): 50g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp
- Dried oregano: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add zucchini and bell pepper, sautéing for 5 minutes until soft.
3. Stir in spinach, salt, black pepper, and oregano, and cook until spinach wilts. Remove from heat.
4. In a bowl, mix the ricotta cheese with half of the parmesan cheese.
5. In a small baking dish (e.g., 8x8 inch), spread a thin layer of tomato sauce.
6. Layer lasagna noodles, half of the ricotta mixture, half of the sautéed vegetables, and a layer of mozzarella cheese.
7. Repeat the layers: noodles, remaining ricotta, remaining vegetables, and more tomato sauce.
8. Top with the remaining lasagna noodles, tomato sauce, mozzarella, and remaining parmesan cheese.
9. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes.
10. Remove the foil and bake for an additional 10 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
11. Let the lasagna rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.

## Safety Notes
- Handle the hot baking dish with oven mitts to prevent burns.
