# Beef Fajitas

**⚠️ Allergens**: gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Flank steak: 300g
- Bell peppers (mixed colors): 300g
- Onion: 150g
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Fajita seasoning: 2 tbsp
- Flour tortillas: 4

## Instructions
1. Slice the flank steak, bell peppers, and onion into thin strips.
2. In a bowl, toss the steak strips with 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp fajita seasoning.
3. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the steak and cook until browned, about 5-7 minutes. Remove the steak and set aside.
4. In the same skillet, add the bell peppers and onions. Toss with the remaining 1 tbsp fajita seasoning. Cook until vegetables are tender, about 7 minutes.
5. Return the steak to the skillet and stir to combine. Cook for 1 more minute until heated through.
6. Warm the flour tortillas and serve immediately with the steak and vegetable mixture.

## Safety Notes
- Wash hands, utensils, and cutting boards after handling raw meat. Cook beef to a safe internal temperature.
