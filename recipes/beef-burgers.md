# Beef Burgers

**⚠️ Allergens**: gluten / dairy / eggs
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Ground beef (80% lean): 300g
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp
- Hamburger buns: 120g
- Cheddar cheese: 40g
- Tomato: 100g
- Lettuce: 30g
- Ketchup: 1 tbsp
- Mayonnaise: 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Divide the ground beef into two equal 150g portions and shape them into patties slightly larger than your hamburger buns.
2. Season both sides of each patty evenly with salt and black pepper.
3. Heat a large skillet or grill pan over medium-high heat.
4. Place the patties in the hot skillet and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until a brown crust forms.
5. Flip the patties, immediately place the cheddar cheese on top, and cook for an additional 3 to 4 minutes.
6. Lightly toast the hamburger buns in a separate pan or toaster.
7. Spread mayonnaise and ketchup on the bottom buns, then layer with lettuce, tomato slices, and the cheese-topped patties before adding the top buns.

## Safety Notes
- Cook ground beef to an internal temperature of 160°F (71°C) to prevent foodborne illness.
