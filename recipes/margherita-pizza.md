# Margherita Pizza

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Pizza dough: 400g
- Tomato sauce: 150g
- Fresh mozzarella cheese: 200g
- Fresh basil leaves: 10g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to its highest setting, ideally around 500°F (260°C).
2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface to your desired thickness.
3. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border for the crust.
4. Tear the fresh mozzarella into pieces and scatter them over the sauce.
5. Bake in the preheated oven for 10-12 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly and slightly blistered.
6. Remove from the oven and immediately top with fresh basil leaves.
7. Drizzle with a little olive oil before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling the hot pizza and oven racks to avoid burns.
