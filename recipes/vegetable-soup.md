# Vegetable Soup

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 40 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Onion, chopped: 100g
- Carrots, chopped: 150g
- Celery, chopped: 100g
- Garlic, minced: 2 cloves
- Vegetable broth: 800g
- Diced tomatoes (canned): 400g
- Potatoes, cubed: 200g
- Green beans, cut: 100g
- Dried oregano: 1 tsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp

## Instructions
1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Add the chopped onion, carrots, and celery to the pot and sauté for 5-7 minutes until softened.
3. Stir in the minced garlic and cook for an additional 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Pour in the vegetable broth and diced tomatoes, then add the cubed potatoes, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper.
5. Bring the soup to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover, and let it simmer for 20 minutes.
6. Add the green beans and continue to simmer for 10 more minutes, or until all vegetables are tender.
7. Serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling hot liquids to avoid burns.
