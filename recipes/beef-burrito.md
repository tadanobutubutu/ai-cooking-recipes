# Beef Burrito

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Ground beef: 250 grams
- Taco seasoning: 1 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Large flour tortillas: 2
- Cooked rice: 150 grams
- Black beans (canned, rinsed): 100 grams
- Shredded cheddar cheese: 50 grams
- Sour cream: 2 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add ground beef and cook until browned, about 5-7 minutes.
2. Stir in taco seasoning and cook for another 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
3. Lay the flour tortillas flat and spread a layer of cooked rice on the center of each.
4. Top the rice with the cooked ground beef, black beans, shredded cheddar cheese, and sour cream.
5. Fold the sides of the tortilla inward, then roll from the bottom up to enclose the filling completely.
6. Optional: lightly toast the burrito in the skillet for 1-2 minutes per side until golden.

## Safety Notes
- Cook ground beef to an internal temperature of 160°F (71°C).
