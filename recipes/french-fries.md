# French Fries

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 45 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Russet potatoes: 500g
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
2. Wash and peel the potatoes (optional), then cut them into 1/4-inch thick sticks.
3. Soak the potato sticks in cold water for at least 30 minutes to remove excess starch.
4. Drain the potatoes and dry them thoroughly with a clean kitchen towel.
5. In a large bowl, toss the dried potatoes with olive oil, salt, and black pepper until evenly coated.
6. Spread the potatoes in a single layer on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
7. Bake for 20 minutes, then flip the fries and bake for another 15-20 minutes until golden and crispy.
8. Remove from the oven, let cool slightly, and serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling hot baking sheets and oven.
