# Chicken Tikka Masala

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 40 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Boneless, skinless chicken thighs: 400g
- Plain yogurt: 100g
- Garam masala: 1 tbsp
- Turmeric: 1 tsp
- Cumin: 1 tsp
- Garlic paste: 1 tbsp
- Ginger paste: 1 tbsp
- Canned crushed tomatoes: 400g
- Heavy cream: 100g
- Vegetable oil: 1 tbsp
- Onion, finely chopped: 1 medium

## Instructions
1. In a bowl, mix yogurt, half of the garam masala, turmeric, cumin, half of the garlic paste, and half of the ginger paste. Add the chicken pieces and coat well. Let marinate for at least 15 minutes.
2. Heat oil in a large skillet or pot over medium-high heat. Add the marinated chicken and cook until browned, about 5-6 minutes. Remove the chicken from the pan and set aside.
3. In the same pan, add the chopped onion and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
4. Stir in the remaining garlic paste, ginger paste, and the rest of the garam masala. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Pour in the crushed tomatoes and bring to a simmer. Let it cook for 10 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld.
6. Return the chicken to the pan and stir in the heavy cream. Simmer for another 5-10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce has thickened.
7. Serve hot, optionally garnished with fresh cilantro, alongside rice or naan.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
