# French Onion Soup

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 65 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Yellow onions: 500g
- Unsalted butter: 50g
- Beef broth: 900g
- Baguette slices: 100g
- Gruyère cheese (grated): 150g
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Slice the yellow onions thinly.
2. In a large pot, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the sliced onions and cook for 45 minutes until deeply caramelized, stirring occasionally.
3. Pour the beef broth into the pot, season with salt and black pepper, and bring to a simmer. Cook for 15 minutes.
4. Preheat the broiler. Ladle the soup into oven-safe bowls.
5. Top each bowl with toasted baguette slices and a generous amount of grated Gruyère cheese.
6. Broil for 3-5 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
7. Serve carefully.

## Safety Notes
- Handle oven-safe bowls with care as they will be extremely hot after broiling.
