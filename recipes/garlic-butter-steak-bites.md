# Garlic Butter Steak Bites

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / soy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Steak (sirloin or ribeye): 450g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Butter: 3 tbsp
- Garlic (minced): 4 cloves
- Soy sauce: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Cut the steak into 1-inch bite-sized cubes. Season generously with salt and pepper.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over high heat until very hot.
3. Add the steak bites in a single layer and sear for 2-3 minutes until a brown crust forms. Flip and cook for another 1 minute.
4. Reduce the heat to medium. Add the butter, minced garlic, and soy sauce to the skillet.
5. Cook for 1-2 minutes, stirring continuously, until the garlic is fragrant and the steak is coated in the butter sauce. Remove from heat immediately.

## Safety Notes
- Consuming raw or undercooked meats may increase your risk of foodborne illness. Adjust cooking time for your preferred level of doneness.
