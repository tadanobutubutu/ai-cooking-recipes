# Greek Salad

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Cucumbers: 200 grams
- Tomatoes: 200 grams
- Red onion: 50 grams
- Kalamata olives: 50 grams
- Feta cheese: 100 grams
- Olive oil: 3 tbsp
- Dried oregano: 1 tsp
- Salt: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Chop the cucumbers, tomatoes, and red onion into bite-sized pieces.
2. In a large bowl, combine the chopped vegetables with the Kalamata olives.
3. Cut the feta cheese into cubes and add it to the bowl.
4. Drizzle the olive oil over the salad.
5. Sprinkle with dried oregano and salt.
6. Toss gently to combine all ingredients.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when using a sharp knife to chop vegetables.
