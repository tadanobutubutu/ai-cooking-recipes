# Chicken Piccata

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken breast (boneless, skinless): 300g
- All-purpose flour: 2 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Unsalted butter: 2 tbsp
- Chicken broth: 100ml
- Lemon juice: 2 tbsp
- Capers (drained): 1 tbsp
- Fresh parsley (chopped): 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp

## Instructions
1. Slice the chicken breast in half horizontally to create two thin cutlets.
2. Season the chicken cutlets with salt and pepper, then coat them evenly in the all-purpose flour.
3. Heat the olive oil and 1 tbsp of butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
4. Add the chicken cutlets and cook for 3-4 minutes on each side until golden brown and cooked through. Remove chicken from the skillet and set aside.
5. In the same skillet, add the chicken broth, lemon juice, and capers. Bring to a simmer and scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan.
6. Let the sauce reduce for about 3 minutes, then stir in the remaining 1 tbsp of butter.
7. Return the chicken to the skillet, spoon the sauce over it, and simmer for 1 more minute.
8. Garnish with chopped fresh parsley before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
- Handle hot oil and butter carefully to avoid splattering.
