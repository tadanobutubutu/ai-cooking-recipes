# Chicken Pot Pie

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 60 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- cooked chicken breast, diced: 250 grams
- frozen mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, corn): 150 grams
- unsalted butter: 45 grams
- all-purpose flour: 30 grams
- chicken broth: 240 grams
- heavy cream: 60 grams
- salt: 1 tsp
- black pepper: 0.5 tsp
- pre-made pie crust: 250 grams

## Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. In a large skillet, melt the butter over medium heat.
3. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute until it forms a paste (roux).
4. Gradually whisk in the chicken broth and heavy cream until smooth.
5. Bring the mixture to a simmer and cook until thickened, about 5 minutes.
6. Stir in the diced chicken, mixed vegetables, salt, and pepper. Cook for another 2 minutes until heated through.
7. Pour the chicken mixture into a small baking dish or two individual ramekins.
8. Cut the pre-made pie crust to fit and place it over the baking dish, crimping the edges to seal. Cut small slits in the top to allow steam to escape.
9. Bake for 25-30 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbly.
10. Let cool for 5 minutes before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure cooked chicken has reached an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C) if cooking from raw.
- Handle hot baking dishes with care when removing from the oven.
