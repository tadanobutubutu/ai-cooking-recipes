# Beef Fried Rice

**⚠️ Allergens**: soy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- cooked white rice (preferably day-old): 400 grams
- beef sirloin (thinly sliced): 200 grams
- soy sauce: 2 tbsp
- vegetable oil: 2 tbsp
- garlic (minced): 2 tsp
- onion (diced): 50 grams
- frozen mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn): 100 grams
- sesame oil: 1 tsp
- salt: 1 tsp
- black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. In a small bowl, marinate the sliced beef with 1 tbsp of soy sauce and black pepper. Let it sit for 5 minutes.
2. Heat 1 tbsp of vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat. Add the marinated beef and stir-fry until browned, about 3-4 minutes. Remove the beef and set aside.
3. In the same wok, add the remaining 1 tbsp of vegetable oil. Sauté the minced garlic and diced onion until fragrant and soft.
4. Add the frozen mixed vegetables and stir-fry for 2 minutes until heated through.
5. Add the cooked rice to the wok, breaking up any clumps. Stir-fry for 3 minutes.
6. Return the cooked beef to the wok. Add the remaining 1 tbsp of soy sauce, sesame oil, and salt. Toss everything together until well combined and the rice is evenly coated.
7. Serve hot and enjoy your homemade beef fried rice.

## Safety Notes
- Handle raw meat safely to avoid cross-contamination.
