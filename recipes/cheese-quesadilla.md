# Cheese Quesadilla

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 10 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Flour tortillas: 4
- Shredded cheese (e.g., cheddar or Monterey Jack): 200 grams
- Butter: 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Heat a large skillet over medium heat and melt half of the butter.
2. Place one tortilla in the skillet and sprinkle 100 grams of shredded cheese evenly over it.
3. Top with a second tortilla and cook for 2-3 minutes until the bottom is golden brown and crispy.
4. Carefully flip the quesadilla and cook for another 2-3 minutes until the cheese is fully melted and the second side is golden brown.
5. Remove from the skillet, let it cool for a minute, and cut into wedges.
6. Repeat the process with the remaining butter, tortillas, and cheese to make the second serving.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when flipping the quesadilla to prevent the hot melted cheese from spilling out.
- Ensure the skillet handle is turned away from the edge of the stove to avoid accidental spills.
