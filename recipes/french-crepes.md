# French Crepes

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / eggs / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- All-purpose flour: 120 grams
- Milk: 240 grams
- Eggs: 100 grams
- Unsalted butter (melted): 30 grams
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Granulated sugar (optional): 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. In a blender or large bowl, combine the flour, milk, eggs, melted butter, salt, and sugar.
2. Blend or whisk until the batter is completely smooth and no lumps remain.
3. Heat a non-stick skillet or crepe pan over medium heat and lightly coat with a little butter or oil.
4. Pour about 3 to 4 tablespoons of batter into the center of the pan, swirling the pan immediately to spread the batter into a thin, even circle.
5. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes until the edges start to lift and the bottom is lightly golden.
6. Gently flip the crepe and cook the other side for another 30 seconds to 1 minute.
7. Repeat with the remaining batter. Serve warm with your favorite fillings.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when flipping the thin crepes to avoid tearing or burning your fingers on the hot pan.
