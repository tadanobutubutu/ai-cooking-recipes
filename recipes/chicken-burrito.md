# Chicken Burrito

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Boneless skinless chicken breasts, diced: 300 gram
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Taco seasoning: 2 tbsp
- Flour tortillas: 150 gram
- Cooked white rice: 200 gram
- Black beans, rinsed: 150 gram
- Shredded cheddar cheese: 100 gram
- Salsa: 4 tbsp
- Sour cream: 2 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
2. Add diced chicken and taco seasoning. Cook until chicken is browned and cooked through, about 8-10 minutes.
3. Warm the flour tortillas in a microwave or dry skillet.
4. Lay the tortillas flat and divide the cooked rice, black beans, cooked chicken, and shredded cheese evenly between them.
5. Top with salsa and sour cream.
6. Fold the sides of the tortillas inward, then roll up tightly to form a burrito.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
