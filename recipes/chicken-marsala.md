# Chicken Marsala

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken breasts (boneless, skinless): 300 grams
- All-purpose flour: 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Unsalted butter: 2 tbsp
- Cremini mushrooms (sliced): 150 grams
- Marsala wine: 8 tbsp
- Chicken broth: 8 tbsp
- Fresh parsley (chopped): 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Place chicken breasts between two pieces of plastic wrap and pound to an even 1/2-inch thickness.
2. In a shallow dish, mix the flour, salt, and black pepper. Dredge the chicken breasts in the flour mixture, shaking off any excess.
3. In a large skillet, heat the olive oil and 1 tablespoon of butter over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook for about 4-5 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through. Transfer chicken to a plate.
4. In the same skillet, add the remaining 1 tablespoon of butter. Add the sliced mushrooms and sauté for about 5 minutes until browned and their liquid has evaporated.
5. Pour in the Marsala wine and chicken broth, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Bring the mixture to a simmer and let it cook for 5-7 minutes until the sauce has reduced and slightly thickened.
6. Return the chicken to the skillet, spooning the sauce over it. Let it simmer for another 2-3 minutes until heated through.
7. Garnish with chopped fresh parsley before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
