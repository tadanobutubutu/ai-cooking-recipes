# Pepperoni Pizza

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Pizza dough: 400g
- Pizza sauce: 120g
- Mozzarella cheese (shredded): 200g
- Pepperoni slices: 50g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C). If using a pizza stone, place it in the oven to heat up.
2. Stretch or roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface to your desired thickness.
3. Transfer the dough to a pizza pan or parchment paper.
4. Brush the edges of the dough with olive oil.
5. Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border for the crust.
6. Sprinkle the shredded mozzarella cheese evenly over the sauce.
7. Arrange the pepperoni slices on top of the cheese.
8. Bake in the preheated oven for 12-15 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly and slightly browned.
9. Remove from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes before slicing.

## Safety Notes
- The oven and baking pan will be extremely hot; use oven mitts when handling.
- Be cautious of hot cheese when slicing and eating the pizza.
