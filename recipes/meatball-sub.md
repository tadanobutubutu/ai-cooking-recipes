# Meatball Sub

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Meatballs (frozen or pre-cooked): 300g
- Marinara sauce: 300g
- Sub rolls: 2
- Provolone cheese: 4 slices

## Instructions
1. In a saucepan over medium heat, combine the meatballs and marinara sauce.
2. Cover and simmer for 15 minutes, or until the meatballs are heated through.
3. Preheat your oven's broiler.
4. Slice the sub rolls open and place them on a baking sheet.
5. Spoon the meatballs and sauce evenly onto the rolls.
6. Top each sub with 2 slices of provolone cheese.
7. Broil for 2-3 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
8. Serve immediately.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure meatballs are heated to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
