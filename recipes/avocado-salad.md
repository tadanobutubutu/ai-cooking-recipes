# Avocado Salad

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 10 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Avocados: 300g
- Cherry tomatoes: 150g
- Red onion: 50g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Lemon juice: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1/2 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. Halve and pit the avocados, then slice them into cubes.
2. Halve the cherry tomatoes and thinly slice the red onion.
3. In a bowl, combine the avocados, tomatoes, and red onion.
4. Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice, then season with salt and pepper.
5. Toss gently to combine.

## Safety Notes
- Wash all produce thoroughly before preparing.
