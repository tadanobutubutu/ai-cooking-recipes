# Turkey Burgers

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Ground turkey: 450g
- Breadcrumbs: 30g
- Egg: 1 large
- Garlic powder: 1 tsp
- Onion powder: 1 tsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Hamburger buns: 2

## Instructions
1. In a large bowl, mix the ground turkey, breadcrumbs, egg, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and black pepper until well combined.
2. Divide the mixture into two equal portions and shape them into patties.
3. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
4. Add the turkey patties to the skillet and cook for about 5-6 minutes on each side, or until browned and cooked through.
5. Serve the turkey burgers on hamburger buns with your favorite toppings.

## Safety Notes
- Cook turkey to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C) to ensure it is safe to eat.
