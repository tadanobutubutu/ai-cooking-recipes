# Roasted Carrots

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Carrots: 450g
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Peel the carrots and cut them diagonally into 1-inch thick slices.
3. Toss the carrots with olive oil, salt, and black pepper in a bowl.
4. Spread the carrots evenly on a baking sheet.
5. Roast for 20-25 minutes until the carrots are tender and the edges are slightly browned.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling the hot baking sheet from the oven.
