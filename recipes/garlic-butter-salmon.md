# Garlic Butter Salmon

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Salmon fillets: 400 grams
- Butter: 3 tbsp
- Garlic (minced): 1 tbsp
- Lemon juice: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp
- Parsley (chopped): 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Melt the butter in a small pan and sauté the minced garlic until fragrant.
3. Place the salmon fillets on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
4. Pour the garlic butter over the salmon and drizzle with lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the salmon flakes easily with a fork.
6. Garnish with chopped parsley before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook salmon to an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C).
