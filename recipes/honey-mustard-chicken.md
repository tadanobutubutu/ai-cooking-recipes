# Honey Mustard Chicken

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken breasts: 400 grams
- Honey: 3 tbsp
- Dijon mustard: 2 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
2. In a small bowl, whisk together the honey, Dijon mustard, olive oil, salt, and black pepper.
3. Place the chicken breasts in a baking dish.
4. Pour the honey mustard mixture over the chicken breasts, making sure they are well coated.
5. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through and the internal temperature reaches 165°F (75°C).
6. Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
