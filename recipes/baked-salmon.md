# Baked Salmon

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Salmon fillets: 300g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Lemon juice: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp
- Garlic powder: 1/2 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Pat the salmon fillets dry with a paper towel and place them on the prepared baking sheet.
3. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, salt, black pepper, and garlic powder.
4. Brush the mixture evenly over the top of the salmon fillets.
5. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, or until the salmon is opaque and flakes easily with a fork.

## Safety Notes
- Cook salmon to an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C).
