# Roasted Potatoes

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 45 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Potatoes (Yukon Gold or Russet): 500 grams
- Olive Oil: 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black Pepper: 0.5 tsp
- Garlic Powder: 1 tsp
- Paprika: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Wash the potatoes thoroughly and cut them into 1-inch cubes. You can leave the skin on for extra texture.
3. In a large bowl, combine the potato cubes, olive oil, salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and paprika. Toss well to ensure all potato pieces are evenly coated.
4. Spread the coated potatoes in a single layer on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
5. Roast in the preheated oven for 30-35 minutes, or until the potatoes are golden brown and crispy on the outside, and fork-tender on the inside. Toss them halfway through the cooking time for even browning.
6. Remove from the oven and let cool slightly before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling the hot baking sheet and opening the oven door, as steam may escape.
