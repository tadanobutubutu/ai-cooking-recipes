# Garlic Mashed Potatoes

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Potatoes (Yukon Gold or Russet): 500g
- Garlic cloves, minced: 3
- Butter: 3 tbsp
- Milk or heavy cream: 100ml
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp

## Instructions
1. Peel and cut the potatoes into chunks.
2. Boil the potatoes in a large pot of salted water for 15-20 minutes until fork-tender.
3. Drain the potatoes and return them to the pot.
4. In a small saucepan, melt the butter and gently sauté the minced garlic for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
5. Add the milk to the butter and garlic, and heat until warm.
6. Pour the warm milk mixture over the potatoes and mash until smooth and creamy.
7. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when draining the hot potatoes and boiling water.
