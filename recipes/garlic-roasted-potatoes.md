# Garlic Roasted Potatoes

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 45 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Potatoes: 500 grams
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Garlic powder: 1 tsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp
- Fresh parsley: 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Wash the potatoes and cut them into bite-sized cubes.
3. In a large bowl, toss the potato cubes with olive oil, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper until evenly coated.
4. Spread the potatoes in a single layer on a baking sheet.
5. Roast in the preheated oven for 35-40 minutes, tossing halfway through, until golden brown and crispy.
6. Remove from the oven and garnish with freshly chopped parsley before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Use oven mitts when handling the hot baking sheet.
- Ensure potatoes are fully cooked and tender when pierced with a fork.
