# Honey Garlic Chicken

**⚠️ Allergens**: soy / dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken breast (boneless, skinless): 400 grams
- Cornstarch: 2 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Butter: 1 tbsp
- Garlic (minced): 3 cloves
- Honey: 3 tbsp
- Soy sauce: 2 tbsp
- Apple cider vinegar: 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Cut the chicken breast into bite-sized pieces and toss with cornstarch until evenly coated.
2. Heat the olive oil and butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Add the chicken pieces and cook for 5-7 minutes until golden brown and cooked through.
4. Reduce the heat to low, then add the minced garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. In a small bowl, whisk together the honey, soy sauce, and apple cider vinegar.
6. Pour the sauce into the skillet and stir to coat the chicken evenly. Simmer for 1-2 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly.
7. Serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
