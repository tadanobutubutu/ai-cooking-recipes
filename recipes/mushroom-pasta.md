# Mushroom Pasta

**⚠️ Allergens**: gluten / dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Spaghetti: 200g
- Button mushrooms (sliced): 150g
- Garlic (minced): 2 cloves
- Heavy cream: 100ml
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp

## Instructions
1. Boil a large pot of salted water and cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions until al dente. Drain and set aside.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Add the minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Add the sliced mushrooms to the skillet and cook for 5-7 minutes until they are browned and tender.
5. Stir in the heavy cream, salt, and black pepper. Reduce the heat to medium-low and let the sauce simmer for 2-3 minutes until slightly thickened.
6. Toss the cooked spaghetti into the sauce, ensuring the pasta is evenly coated.
7. Serve hot and enjoy.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling hot water and draining the pasta to avoid burns.
