# Baked Ziti

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 45 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Ziti pasta: 225 grams
- Marinara sauce: 350 grams
- Ricotta cheese: 125 grams
- Mozzarella cheese: 150 grams
- Parmesan cheese: 30 grams
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Cook the ziti pasta according to package instructions until al dente. Drain and toss with olive oil.
3. In a bowl, mix the cooked pasta with marinara sauce and ricotta cheese.
4. Transfer half of the pasta mixture to a baking dish. Top with half of the mozzarella and parmesan cheese.
5. Add the remaining pasta mixture and top with the remaining cheese.
6. Bake for 20-25 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
7. Let it cool for 5 minutes before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Handle hot baking dishes with oven mitts to prevent burns.
