# Garlic Butter Noodles

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Spaghetti or preferred pasta: 200g
- Unsalted butter: 4 tbsp
- Garlic (minced): 4 cloves
- Parmesan cheese (grated): 30g
- Fresh parsley (chopped): 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1/2 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. Boil a large pot of salted water. Cook pasta according to package instructions until al dente. Reserve 1/4 cup of pasta water before draining.
2. In a large skillet, melt butter over medium heat. Add minced garlic and sauté for 1-2 minutes until fragrant but not browned.
3. Add the cooked pasta to the skillet, tossing to coat in the garlic butter. If the sauce is too dry, add a splash of the reserved pasta water.
4. Remove from heat and stir in the grated Parmesan cheese and chopped parsley. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
5. Serve immediately with extra Parmesan on top if desired.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful not to burn the garlic, as it will become bitter.
