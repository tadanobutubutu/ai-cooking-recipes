# Chicken Salad Sandwich

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Cooked chicken breast: 200g
- Mayonnaise: 3 tbsp
- Celery (finely chopped): 40g
- Red onion (finely chopped): 20g
- Lemon juice: 1 tsp
- Bread: 4 slices

## Instructions
1. Shred or finely chop the cooked chicken breast.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine the chicken, mayonnaise, celery, red onion, and lemon juice. Mix well.
3. Divide the chicken salad mixture evenly onto 2 slices of bread.
4. Top with the remaining 2 slices of bread to complete the sandwiches.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure cooked chicken is fresh and has been stored safely in the refrigerator if prepared ahead of time.
