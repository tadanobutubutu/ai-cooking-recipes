# Shrimp Tacos

**⚠️ Allergens**: shellfish / gluten / dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Small shrimp, peeled and deveined: 250 g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Chili powder: 1 tsp
- Cumin: 0.5 tsp
- Garlic powder: 0.5 tsp
- Salt: 0.5 tsp
- Small flour tortillas: 4
- Shredded cabbage: 100 g
- Lime (cut into wedges): 1
- Fresh cilantro, chopped: 2 tbsp
- Sour cream: 2 tbsp

## Instructions
1. In a bowl, toss the shrimp with olive oil, chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, and salt.
2. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the shrimp and cook for 2-3 minutes per side until pink and cooked through.
3. Warm the tortillas in the microwave for 20 seconds or in a dry skillet.
4. Assemble the tacos by placing a layer of shredded cabbage on each tortilla.
5. Top with the cooked shrimp, a dollop of sour cream, and a sprinkle of cilantro.
6. Serve with lime wedges on the side.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure shrimp is fully cooked until opaque and pink.
