# Beef Stroganoff

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Beef Sirloin Steak: 300g
- Button Mushrooms: 150g
- Onion (medium): 1/2
- Butter: 2 tbsp
- All-Purpose Flour: 1 tbsp
- Beef Broth: 150ml
- Sour Cream: 100g
- Dijon Mustard: 1 tsp
- Salt: 1/2 tsp
- Black Pepper: 1/4 tsp
- Egg Noodles (dry): 150g
- Olive Oil: 1 tbsp
- Fresh Parsley (chopped): 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Slice the beef sirloin across the grain into thin strips. Season with salt and black pepper.
2. Slice the mushrooms and finely chop the onion.
3. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil and cook the egg noodles according to package directions. Drain and set aside.
4. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the beef strips and sear quickly for 1-2 minutes per side until browned. Remove the beef from the skillet and set aside on a plate.
5. In the same skillet, lower the heat to medium and melt the butter. Add the chopped onion and cook for 3-4 minutes until softened.
6. Add the sliced mushrooms and cook for another 4-5 minutes until browned.
7. Sprinkle the flour over the mushroom and onion mixture, stirring constantly for 1 minute.
8. Gradually pour in the beef broth, stirring constantly to avoid lumps. Bring to a simmer and let it thicken slightly for 2-3 minutes.
9. Reduce the heat to low. Stir in the sour cream and Dijon mustard. Do not let the sauce boil after adding the sour cream to prevent it from curdling.
10. Return the browned beef (and any resting juices) to the skillet, stirring to combine and heat through for 1-2 minutes.
11. Serve the beef stroganoff over the cooked egg noodles, garnished with chopped fresh parsley.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure beef is cooked to an internal temperature of at least 145°F (63°C) with a 3-minute rest for medium-rare, or higher depending on your preference.
- Be careful not to let the sauce boil after adding the sour cream, as it can cause the dairy to separate and curdle.
