# Cacio e Pepe

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Spaghetti: 200g
- Pecorino Romano cheese, grated: 100g
- Black pepper, freshly cracked: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil. Add the spaghetti and cook until al dente.
2. While the pasta cooks, place the grated Pecorino Romano and freshly cracked black pepper in a large bowl.
3. Once the pasta is done, reserve about 1 cup of the pasta cooking water.
4. Transfer the hot pasta directly to the bowl with the cheese and pepper.
5. Add a splash of the hot pasta water and toss vigorously until a creamy sauce forms, adding more water if necessary.
6. Serve immediately with extra grated cheese and black pepper on top.

## Safety Notes
- Handle hot water carefully when transferring pasta and reserving pasta water to avoid burns.
