# Grilled Chicken

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken breasts: 400g
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp
- Garlic powder: 1 tsp
- Paprika: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat the grill to medium-high heat.
2. In a small bowl, mix olive oil, salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and paprika.
3. Rub the spice mixture evenly over the chicken breasts.
4. Grill the chicken for 6-8 minutes on each side.
5. Remove from grill and let rest for 5 minutes before slicing and serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
