# Classic Waffles

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / eggs / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- All-purpose flour: 250 grams
- Baking powder: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Granulated sugar: 2 tbsp
- Milk: 240 grams
- Eggs (beaten): 100 grams
- Melted butter: 60 grams

## Instructions
1. Preheat the waffle iron.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the milk, eggs, and melted butter.
4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined.
5. Cook the batter in the waffle iron according to the manufacturer's instructions until golden brown.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when using hot cooking equipment like a waffle iron to prevent burns.
