# Beef Stir Fry

**⚠️ Allergens**: soy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Flank steak, thinly sliced: 300g
- Broccoli florets: 150g
- Bell pepper, sliced: 100g
- Soy sauce: 2 tbsp
- Vegetable oil: 1 tbsp
- Garlic, minced: 1 tsp
- Ginger, minced: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. In a small bowl, mix the soy sauce, minced garlic, and minced ginger.
2. Heat vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Add the thinly sliced flank steak and cook until browned, about 3-4 minutes. Remove steak and set aside.
4. In the same wok, add broccoli florets and bell pepper. Stir-fry for 4-5 minutes until tender-crisp.
5. Return the steak to the wok. Pour the soy sauce mixture over the meat and vegetables.
6. Toss everything together and cook for another 1-2 minutes until heated through.
7. Serve hot over rice or noodles.

## Safety Notes
- Cook beef to a minimum internal temperature of 145°F (63°C) and allow to rest for 3 minutes.
- Ensure all vegetables are thoroughly washed before use.
