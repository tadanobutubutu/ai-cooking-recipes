# Egg Drop Soup

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / soy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken broth: 500g
- Eggs: 2 large (about 100g)
- Soy sauce: 1 tbsp
- Cornstarch: 1 tbsp
- Water: 2 tbsp
- Sesame oil: 1 tsp
- Green onions: 2 tbsp (chopped)
- Salt: 1/2 tsp
- White pepper: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. In a small bowl, whisk together the cornstarch and water until smooth to create a slurry. Set aside.
2. In another small bowl, lightly beat the eggs.
3. In a medium pot, bring the chicken broth to a boil over medium-high heat.
4. Stir in the soy sauce, salt, white pepper, and sesame oil.
5. Reduce the heat to low so the broth is at a gentle simmer.
6. Slowly stir the cornstarch slurry into the broth and cook for 1 minute until the soup thickens slightly.
7. Use a ladle or spoon to stir the soup in a circular motion to create a whirlpool.
8. Slowly pour the beaten eggs into the whirlpool in a thin stream.
9. Let the soup sit untouched for a few seconds to allow the eggs to set into ribbons, then gently stir.
10. Remove from heat, garnish with chopped green onions, and serve immediately.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure eggs are cooked through by pouring them into simmering hot broth.
