# Egg Salad Sandwich

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Large eggs: 200 grams
- Mayonnaise: 3 tbsp
- Dijon mustard: 1 tsp
- Finely chopped celery: 20 grams
- Salt: 1/4 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp
- Sandwich bread: 150 grams
- Lettuce leaves: 20 grams

## Instructions
1. Place eggs in a saucepan, cover with water, and bring to a boil.
2. Remove from heat, cover, and let sit for 10 minutes.
3. Transfer eggs to an ice bath to cool, then peel and chop them.
4. In a bowl, mix the chopped eggs with mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, celery, salt, and black pepper.
5. Toast the bread slices if desired.
6. Assemble the sandwiches by dividing the egg salad and lettuce between the bread slices.

## Safety Notes
- Keep the egg salad refrigerated if not consuming immediately.
