# Guacamole

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 10 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Avocados: 300 grams
- Onion: 50 grams
- Tomato: 50 grams
- Cilantro: 2 tbsp
- Lime juice: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Halve the avocados, remove the pit, and scoop the flesh into a bowl.
2. Mash the avocado flesh with a fork until it reaches your desired consistency.
3. Finely dice the onion and tomato, and roughly chop the cilantro.
4. Add the onion, tomato, cilantro, lime juice, and salt to the mashed avocado.
5. Stir well to combine all ingredients.

## Safety Notes
- Wash all fresh produce thoroughly before preparing.
