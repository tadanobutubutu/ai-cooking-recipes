# Mango Sticky Rice

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 45 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Glutinous Rice: 200 gram
- Coconut Milk: 200 gram
- Sugar: 3 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Ripe Mango: 300 gram
- Sesame Seeds: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Rinse the glutinous rice thoroughly and soak it in water for at least 30 minutes.
2. Drain the rice and steam it for about 20 to 25 minutes until tender.
3. In a small saucepan, heat the coconut milk, sugar, and salt over medium heat until the sugar dissolves. Do not let it boil.
4. Pour the warm coconut milk mixture over the cooked rice, stir gently, and let it sit for 15 minutes to absorb the liquid.
5. Peel and slice the ripe mango into bite-sized pieces.
6. Serve the sticky rice with the sliced mango and sprinkle with sesame seeds.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling the hot steamer and steam.
