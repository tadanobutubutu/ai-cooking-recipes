# Tofu Scramble

**⚠️ Allergens**: soy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 10 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Firm tofu: 400g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Nutritional yeast: 2 tbsp
- Turmeric powder: 1 tsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Crumble the firm tofu into bite-sized pieces using your hands or a fork.
2. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
3. Add the crumbled tofu to the pan and cook for 3-4 minutes until slightly browned.
4. Sprinkle nutritional yeast, turmeric powder, salt, and black pepper over the tofu.
5. Stir well to coat the tofu evenly with the spices and cook for another 2-3 minutes until heated through.
6. Serve hot, optionally with toast or avocado.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure the tofu is heated thoroughly before serving.
