# Roasted Brussels Sprouts

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Brussels sprouts (trimmed and halved): 450 grams
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. In a bowl, toss the halved Brussels sprouts with olive oil, salt, and black pepper.
3. Spread the Brussels sprouts in a single layer on a baking sheet.
4. Roast in the preheated oven for 20 to 25 minutes, shaking the pan halfway through, until crisp on the outside and tender on the inside.

## Safety Notes
- Use oven mitts when handling the hot baking sheet.
