# Beef Quesadilla

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Ground beef: 225 grams
- Flour tortillas: 100 grams
- Shredded cheddar cheese: 110 grams
- Taco seasoning: 1 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Diced onion: 50 grams

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
2. Add diced onion and cook until softened, about 3 minutes.
3. Add ground beef to the skillet and cook until browned and fully cooked. Drain excess fat.
4. Stir in taco seasoning and mix thoroughly. Remove the beef mixture from the skillet and set aside.
5. In the same skillet, place one tortilla and sprinkle half of the cheese over one side.
6. Spread half of the beef mixture over the cheese, then fold the tortilla in half.
7. Cook for 2-3 minutes per side until golden brown and the cheese is melted. Repeat with the second tortilla.
8. Remove from the skillet, cut into wedges, and serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Cook ground beef to an internal temperature of 160°F (71°C).
