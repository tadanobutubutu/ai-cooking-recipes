# Spaghetti Bolognese

**⚠️ Allergens**: gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 45 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Spaghetti pasta: 200g
- Ground beef: 250g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Onion, finely chopped: 100g
- Garlic, minced: 2 cloves
- Tomato paste: 2 tbsp
- Crushed tomatoes: 400g
- Dried oregano: 1 tsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and minced garlic, sautéing until soft and translucent (about 5 minutes).
2. Add the ground beef to the pan, breaking it apart with a spoon. Cook until browned and fully cooked through.
3. Stir in the tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper.
4. Reduce the heat to low, cover the pan, and let the sauce simmer for 20-30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. While the sauce is simmering, bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions until al dente.
6. Drain the spaghetti and serve topped with the Bolognese sauce.

## Safety Notes
- Cook ground beef to an internal temperature of 160°F (71°C).
