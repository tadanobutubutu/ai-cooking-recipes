# Vegan Chili

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 30 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Canned kidney beans (drained): 400g
- Canned diced tomatoes: 400g
- Onion (chopped): 150g
- Garlic (minced): 1 tbsp
- Chili powder: 1 tbsp
- Cumin powder: 1 tsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Water: 120g

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a pot over medium heat.
2. Add chopped onions and garlic, sautéing until softened.
3. Stir in chili powder and cumin, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Add kidney beans, diced tomatoes, water, and salt.
5. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat and cook for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling hot liquids to avoid burns.
