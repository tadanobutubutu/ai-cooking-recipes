# Chicken and Rice

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 40 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken breast: 400 grams
- White rice: 200 grams
- Chicken broth: 400 grams
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp
- Garlic powder: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. In a medium pot, heat the olive oil over medium-high heat.
2. Season the chicken breast with salt, black pepper, and garlic powder.
3. Cook the chicken in the pot until golden brown on all sides, about 5 minutes per side.
4. Remove the chicken from the pot and set aside.
5. Add the white rice to the pot and toast for 1 minute, stirring frequently.
6. Pour in the chicken broth and bring to a boil.
7. Return the chicken to the pot, reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes or until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed.
8. Remove from heat and let rest for 5 minutes before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
