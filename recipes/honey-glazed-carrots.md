# Honey Glazed Carrots

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Carrots, peeled and sliced: 300 grams
- Honey: 2 tbsp
- Unsalted butter: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1/2 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. In a large skillet, melt the butter over medium heat.
2. Add the sliced carrots and cook for about 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tender.
3. Add the honey, salt, and black pepper to the skillet.
4. Cook for an additional 5 minutes, allowing the carrots to glaze and caramelize slightly.
5. Remove from heat and serve warm.

## Safety Notes
- Be cautious when handling the hot skillet and melting butter to prevent burns.
