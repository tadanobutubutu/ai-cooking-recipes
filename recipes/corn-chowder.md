# Corn Chowder

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 45 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Potatoes, diced: 400g
- Corn kernels: 300g
- Onion, chopped: 100g
- Bacon, chopped: 100g
- Vegetable broth: 500g
- Heavy cream: 100g
- Butter: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. In a large pot, cook the bacon over medium heat until crispy. Remove bacon and set aside, leaving the fat in the pot.
2. Add the butter and chopped onion to the pot. Sauté until the onion is translucent, about 5 minutes.
3. Stir in the diced potatoes, corn kernels, and vegetable broth. Bring to a boil.
4. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes, or until potatoes are tender.
5. Stir in the heavy cream, cooked bacon, salt, and black pepper. Simmer for an additional 5 minutes.
6. Serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Handle hot soup carefully to avoid burns.
