# Cucumber Salad

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Cucumber: 300g
- Red onion: 50g
- White vinegar: 3 tbsp
- Water: 2 tbsp
- Sugar: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Thinly slice the cucumbers and red onions.
2. In a small bowl, whisk together the white vinegar, water, sugar, salt, and black pepper until the sugar dissolves.
3. Place the sliced cucumbers and red onions in a serving bowl.
4. Pour the dressing over the vegetables and toss to combine.
5. Cover and refrigerate for at least 10 minutes before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Wash cucumbers thoroughly before slicing.
