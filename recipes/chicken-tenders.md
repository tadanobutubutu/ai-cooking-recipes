# Chicken Tenders

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken breast: 400g
- All-purpose flour: 60g
- Eggs: 2
- Panko breadcrumbs: 100g
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp
- Garlic powder: 1 tsp
- Paprika: 1 tsp
- Cooking spray: 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Cut the chicken breast into even strips.
3. Set up three bowls: one with flour, salt, pepper, garlic powder, and paprika; one with beaten eggs; and one with panko breadcrumbs.
4. Coat each chicken strip first in the flour mixture, then dip into the beaten eggs, and finally press into the panko breadcrumbs until fully coated.
5. Place the coated chicken tenders on the prepared baking sheet and lightly spray with cooking spray.
6. Bake for 15-20 minutes, flipping halfway, until the chicken is golden brown and cooked through.

## Safety Notes
- Always wash hands, utensils, and surfaces thoroughly after handling raw chicken.
- Cook chicken to a safe internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
