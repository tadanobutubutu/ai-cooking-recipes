# Baked Sweet Potato

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 50 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Sweet potatoes: 450 grams
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1/2 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Scrub the sweet potatoes clean under running water and pat them dry with a towel.
3. Poke each sweet potato 4-5 times with a fork to allow steam to escape while baking.
4. Rub the sweet potatoes evenly with olive oil, then sprinkle with salt and black pepper.
5. Place the sweet potatoes on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper or aluminum foil.
6. Bake for 45-50 minutes, or until tender when pierced with a fork.
7. Let cool slightly before slicing open to serve.

## Safety Notes
- Use oven mitts when handling hot baking sheets and pans.
- Allow the sweet potatoes to cool slightly before handling, as they retain a lot of heat and release hot steam when cut open.
