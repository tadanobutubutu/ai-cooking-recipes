# Quinoa Salad

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Quinoa: 100 grams
- Water: 240 grams
- Cherry tomatoes: 100 grams
- Cucumber: 100 grams
- Red onion: 30 grams
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Lemon juice: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1/2 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. Rinse the quinoa under cold water in a fine-mesh strainer.
2. In a medium saucepan, bring the water to a boil. Add the rinsed quinoa and reduce the heat to low. Cover and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all the water is absorbed.
3. Remove the saucepan from the heat and let it sit covered for 5 minutes. Fluff the quinoa with a fork and transfer it to a large bowl to cool.
4. While the quinoa is cooling, halve the cherry tomatoes, dice the cucumber, and finely chop the red onion.
5. Add the tomatoes, cucumber, and red onion to the cooled quinoa.
6. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and black pepper.
7. Pour the dressing over the quinoa salad and toss to combine evenly.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling hot water and the saucepan.
