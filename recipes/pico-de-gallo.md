# Pico de Gallo

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Roma tomatoes, diced: 250 grams
- White onion, finely chopped: 50 grams
- Fresh cilantro, chopped: 15 grams
- Jalapeño, seeded and minced: 15 grams
- Lime juice, fresh: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Wash and dice the Roma tomatoes, and place them in a medium bowl.
2. Finely chop the white onion, cilantro, and jalapeño, and add them to the bowl with the tomatoes.
3. Drizzle the fresh lime juice over the mixture and sprinkle with salt.
4. Stir all the ingredients together until well combined.
5. Let the mixture sit for about 10 minutes to allow the flavors to meld before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Wash hands thoroughly with soap and water after handling jalapeños, and avoid touching your eyes or face.
