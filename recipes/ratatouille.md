# Ratatouille

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 60 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Eggplant: 200g
- Zucchini: 200g
- Bell pepper: 150g
- Tomatoes: 300g
- Onion: 100g
- Garlic: 10g
- Olive oil: 3 tbsp
- Dried thyme: 1 tsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Dice the eggplant, zucchini, bell pepper, tomatoes, and onion into uniform pieces. Mince the garlic.
2. Heat 2 tbsp of olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the onions and garlic, cooking until softened (about 5 minutes).
3. Add the bell pepper and cook for another 3 minutes.
4. Stir in the eggplant, zucchini, and remaining 1 tbsp of olive oil. Cook for 5-7 minutes until vegetables begin to soften.
5. Add the tomatoes, dried thyme, salt, and black pepper. Stir well to combine.
6. Reduce the heat to low, cover the pot, and simmer for 40 minutes, stirring occasionally.
7. Taste and adjust seasoning if necessary. Serve hot or at room temperature.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling the hot pot and simmering liquids to avoid burns.
