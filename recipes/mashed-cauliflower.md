# Mashed Cauliflower

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Cauliflower florets: 450 grams
- Butter: 2 tbsp
- Heavy cream: 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
2. Add the cauliflower florets and cook for 10-15 minutes until very tender.
3. Drain the cauliflower well and return it to the pot to let excess moisture steam off for a minute.
4. Transfer the cauliflower to a food processor or use an immersion blender.
5. Add the butter, heavy cream, salt, and black pepper.
6. Blend until smooth and creamy. Serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling hot liquids and steam while boiling and draining the cauliflower.
