# Chicken Parmesan

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten / eggs
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 45 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Chicken breast: 400 gram
- All-purpose flour: 30 gram
- Eggs: 1
- Breadcrumbs: 60 gram
- Parmesan cheese: 30 gram
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Marinara sauce: 250 gram
- Mozzarella cheese: 100 gram
- Fresh basil: 5 gram

## Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Slice the chicken breasts in half horizontally to create four thinner cutlets, then pound them to an even thickness.
3. Set up a breading station with three shallow dishes: one with flour, one with the beaten egg, and one with a mixture of breadcrumbs and grated Parmesan cheese.
4. Coat each chicken cutlet first in flour, then dip into the egg, and finally coat evenly with the breadcrumb mixture.
5. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Fry the breaded chicken cutlets for about 3-4 minutes per side until golden brown and crispy.
6. Transfer the fried chicken to a baking dish. Top each piece with marinara sauce and shredded mozzarella cheese.
7. Bake in the preheated oven for 10-15 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and bubbly, and the chicken is cooked through.
8. Garnish with chopped fresh basil before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
- Be careful when frying the chicken to avoid hot oil splatters.
