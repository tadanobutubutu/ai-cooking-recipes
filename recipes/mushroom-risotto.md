# Mushroom Risotto

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 40 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Arborio rice: 150 grams
- Mushrooms (cremini or button, sliced): 200 grams
- Vegetable broth: 500 grams
- Onion (finely chopped): 50 grams
- Garlic (minced): 1 tbsp
- Butter: 2 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Parmesan cheese (grated): 30 grams
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil and 1 tbsp of butter in a large pan over medium heat.
2. Add the chopped onion and minced garlic, sautéing until the onion is translucent (about 3-4 minutes).
3. Add the sliced mushrooms to the pan and cook until they release their moisture and brown (about 5-7 minutes).
4. Stir in the Arborio rice, toasting it slightly for 1-2 minutes until the edges are translucent.
5. Gradually add the warm vegetable broth, about 100 grams at a time, stirring frequently. Allow the liquid to absorb before adding more.
6. Continue adding broth and stirring until the rice is creamy and tender, about 20-25 minutes.
7. Remove the pan from heat and stir in the remaining 1 tbsp of butter and grated Parmesan cheese.
8. Season with salt and black pepper to taste. Serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when stirring hot liquids to avoid splashes.
- Ensure mushrooms are thoroughly cooked.
