# Beef Enchiladas

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 40 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Ground beef: 300 grams
- Enchilada sauce: 250 grams
- Shredded cheddar cheese: 100 grams
- Corn tortillas: 150 grams
- Diced onions: 50 grams
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add diced onions and cook until softened.
3. Add ground beef, salt, and pepper to the pan. Cook until the beef is browned and no longer pink. Drain excess fat.
4. Stir 50 grams of enchilada sauce into the beef mixture.
5. Warm the corn tortillas slightly in the microwave to make them pliable.
6. Spoon a portion of the beef mixture down the center of each tortilla, roll them up, and place them seam-side down in a baking dish.
7. Pour the remaining enchilada sauce evenly over the rolled tortillas.
8. Sprinkle the shredded cheddar cheese evenly on top.
9. Bake for 15-20 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
10. Let cool for 5 minutes before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook ground beef to an internal temperature of 160°F (71°C).
