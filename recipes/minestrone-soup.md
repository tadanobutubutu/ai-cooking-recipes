# Minestrone Soup

**⚠️ Allergens**: gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 40 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Yellow onion, chopped: 100 grams
- Carrots, diced: 100 grams
- Celery, diced: 100 grams
- Garlic, minced: 1 tbsp
- Tomato paste: 1 tbsp
- Diced tomatoes (canned): 400 grams
- Vegetable broth: 500 grams
- Kidney beans (canned, rinsed): 200 grams
- Small pasta (e.g., ditalini or macaroni): 50 grams
- Spinach, chopped: 50 grams
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Add onion, carrots, and celery. Sauté for 5-7 minutes until vegetables are softened.
3. Stir in minced garlic and tomato paste, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Add diced tomatoes, vegetable broth, salt, and black pepper. Bring to a simmer.
5. Cover and simmer for 15 minutes, or until the carrots and celery are tender.
6. Stir in the kidney beans and pasta. Simmer for another 10 minutes until pasta is cooked.
7. Stir in the chopped spinach and cook for 2 minutes until wilted.
8. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed, then serve hot.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling hot liquids to avoid burns.
