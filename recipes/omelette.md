# Omelette

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs / dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 10 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Large eggs: 4
- Whole milk: 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1/4 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp
- Unsalted butter: 1 tbsp
- Shredded cheddar cheese: 50g

## Instructions
1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and add the milk, salt, and black pepper. Whisk vigorously until the mixture is uniform and slightly frothy.
2. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and add the butter, swirling to coat the bottom as it melts.
3. Pour the egg mixture into the skillet. Let it sit for a moment until the edges start to set.
4. Using a spatula, gently push the cooked edges toward the center, allowing the uncooked egg to flow underneath.
5. When the eggs are mostly set but still slightly runny on top, sprinkle the cheddar cheese over one half of the omelette.
6. Fold the other half over the cheese and cook for another minute until the cheese is melted.
7. Slide the omelette onto a plate, cut in half, and serve immediately.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure eggs are cooked thoroughly to reduce the risk of foodborne illness.
