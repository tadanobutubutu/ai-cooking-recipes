# Potato Salad

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Potatoes: 500g
- Mayonnaise: 4 tbsp
- Yellow mustard: 1 tsp
- Hard-boiled eggs: 2
- Celery (diced): 50g
- Red onion (finely diced): 30g
- Salt: 1/2 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. Boil the potatoes in a large pot of salted water until fork-tender, about 15-20 minutes. Drain and let cool completely.
2. Peel and dice the potatoes into bite-sized cubes.
3. Peel and chop the hard-boiled eggs.
4. In a large bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, yellow mustard, salt, and black pepper.
5. Add the diced potatoes, chopped eggs, celery, and red onion to the bowl.
6. Gently fold the mixture until everything is evenly coated with the dressing.
7. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Wash your hands, utensils, and cutting boards after handling eggs.
- Keep the potato salad refrigerated when not being served to prevent bacterial growth.
