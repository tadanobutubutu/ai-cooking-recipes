# Lemon Pepper Chicken

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Boneless skinless chicken breasts: 400g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Lemon pepper seasoning: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Lemon juice: 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat and add olive oil.
2. Season the chicken breasts evenly with lemon pepper seasoning and salt.
3. Cook the chicken in the skillet for 6-8 minutes on each side, or until golden brown and cooked through.
4. Drizzle the cooked chicken with lemon juice before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
