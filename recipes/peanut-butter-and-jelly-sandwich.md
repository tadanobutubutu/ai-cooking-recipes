# Peanut Butter and Jelly Sandwich

**⚠️ Allergens**: nuts / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 5 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Sliced bread: 4 slices
- Peanut butter: 4 tbsp
- Fruit jelly or jam: 4 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Lay out the 4 slices of bread on a clean, flat surface.
2. Using a butter knife, spread 2 tablespoons of peanut butter evenly over one side of 2 slices of bread.
3. Spread 2 tablespoons of fruit jelly or jam evenly over one side of the remaining 2 slices of bread.
4. Carefully place the jelly-covered slices onto the peanut butter-covered slices, sticky sides together, to form 2 sandwiches.
5. Cut each sandwich in half diagonally or straight across, according to preference, and serve immediately.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when using knives to spread ingredients and cut the sandwiches.
