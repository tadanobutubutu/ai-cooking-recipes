# Crispy Baked Tofu

**⚠️ Allergens**: soy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 35 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Extra firm tofu: 400 grams
- Soy sauce: 2 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Cornstarch: 2 tbsp
- Garlic powder: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp

## Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Drain the extra firm tofu and press it for about 10-15 minutes to remove excess moisture.
3. Cut the pressed tofu into bite-sized cubes.
4. In a bowl, toss the tofu cubes with soy sauce and olive oil until evenly coated.
5. Add the cornstarch, garlic powder, and black pepper to the bowl. Toss gently until the tofu is fully coated.
6. Spread the coated tofu cubes in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet.
7. Bake for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway through, until the tofu is golden and crispy on the edges.
8. Serve immediately with your favorite sauce or over rice.

## Safety Notes
- Be careful when handling the hot baking sheet from the oven.
- Ensure the tofu is pressed properly to avoid excessive splattering if pan-frying later instead of baking.
