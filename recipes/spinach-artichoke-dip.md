# Spinach Artichoke Dip

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Cream Cheese: 115 grams
- Sour Cream: 60 grams
- Mayonnaise: 30 grams
- Garlic (minced): 1 tsp
- Parmesan Cheese (grated): 30 grams
- Mozzarella Cheese (shredded): 60 grams
- Frozen Spinach (thawed and drained): 150 grams
- Artichoke Hearts (chopped): 150 grams

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. In a medium bowl, mix together cream cheese, sour cream, mayonnaise, and garlic until smooth.
3. Stir in Parmesan cheese, 30 grams of mozzarella cheese, spinach, and artichoke hearts.
4. Transfer the mixture to a small baking dish and top with the remaining 30 grams of mozzarella cheese.
5. Bake for 20 minutes, or until the cheese is bubbly and golden on top.
6. Serve warm with tortilla chips or toasted bread.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure frozen spinach is fully heated through before serving.
