# Tomato and Egg Stir Fry

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Eggs: 4
- Tomatoes: 400g
- Vegetable oil: 2 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Sugar: 1 tsp
- Scallions: 2 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Beat the eggs in a bowl. Chop the tomatoes into bite-sized wedges and slice the scallions.
2. Heat 1 tbsp of oil in a wok or pan over medium-high heat. Pour in the eggs and scramble until just set. Remove the eggs and set aside.
3. Add the remaining 1 tbsp of oil to the pan. Add the tomatoes and cook until they soften and release their juices, about 3-4 minutes.
4. Add salt and sugar to the tomatoes and stir well.
5. Return the scrambled eggs to the pan. Gently fold the eggs and tomatoes together until heated through.
6. Garnish with scallions and serve immediately.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure eggs are fully cooked if serving to vulnerable individuals.
- Be careful of hot oil splattering when adding eggs to the pan.
