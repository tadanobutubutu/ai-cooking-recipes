# Miso Soup

**⚠️ Allergens**: soy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Dashi stock: 480 grams
- Miso paste: 2 tbsp
- Silken tofu: 150 grams
- Dried wakame seaweed: 1 tbsp
- Green onion: 15 grams

## Instructions
1. In a small bowl, rehydrate the dried wakame seaweed in warm water for 5 minutes, then drain.
2. In a medium saucepan, bring the dashi stock to a gentle simmer over medium heat.
3. Add the cubed silken tofu and rehydrated wakame to the simmering stock and cook for 2 minutes.
4. Turn off the heat or lower it to the minimum setting. Do not let the soup boil once miso is added.
5. Place the miso paste in a ladle, lower it into the stock, and use a small whisk to dissolve the miso completely into the soup.
6. Serve immediately, garnished with chopped green onions.

## Safety Notes
- Do not let the soup boil after adding the miso paste.
