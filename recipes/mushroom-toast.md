# Mushroom Toast

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Sliced mushrooms: 200 grams
- Butter: 2 tbsp
- Minced garlic: 10 grams
- Sourdough bread: 200 grams
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp

## Instructions
1. Slice the sourdough bread and toast it until golden brown.
2. Heat the butter in a pan over medium heat.
3. Add the minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Add the sliced mushrooms to the pan and cook for 5-7 minutes until browned.
5. Season the mushrooms with salt and black pepper.
6. Spoon the cooked mushrooms over the toasted sourdough slices.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure mushrooms are cooked thoroughly before consuming.
- Use caution when handling hot pans and ingredients.
