# Teriyaki Beef

**⚠️ Allergens**: soy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Beef sirloin: 300 gram
- Soy sauce: 4 tbsp
- Brown sugar: 2 tbsp
- Minced garlic: 1 tsp
- Minced ginger: 1 tsp
- Cornstarch: 1 tbsp
- Water: 4 tbsp
- Vegetable oil: 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Slice the beef into thin strips.
2. In a bowl, mix soy sauce, brown sugar, minced garlic, and minced ginger.
3. Heat vegetable oil in a pan over medium-high heat.
4. Add the beef and cook until browned.
5. Pour the soy sauce mixture over the beef and simmer.
6. In a small bowl, mix cornstarch and water.
7. Stir the cornstarch mixture into the pan and cook until the sauce thickens.

## Safety Notes
- Cook beef to an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C).
