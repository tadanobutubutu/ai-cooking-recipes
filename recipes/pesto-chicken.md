# Pesto Chicken

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / nuts
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 25 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Boneless skinless chicken breasts: 450g
- Basil pesto: 4 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1/2 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. Season the chicken breasts evenly with salt and black pepper on both sides.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Add the chicken breasts to the skillet and cook for 5-7 minutes per side, or until golden brown and fully cooked.
4. Reduce the heat to low. Spoon the basil pesto over the chicken breasts, spreading it evenly.
5. Cover the skillet and let it simmer for 1-2 minutes until the pesto is heated through.
6. Remove from heat and let the chicken rest for 3 minutes before slicing and serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C).
