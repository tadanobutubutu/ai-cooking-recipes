# Pork Chops

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Pork chops (bone-in or boneless): 400g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Butter: 2 tbsp
- Garlic powder: 1 tsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.5 tsp

## Instructions
1. Pat the pork chops dry with a paper towel and season both sides with garlic powder, salt, and black pepper.
2. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Add the pork chops and sear for 4-5 minutes on each side until golden brown and cooked through.
4. Reduce the heat to medium-low, add butter to the skillet, and baste the pork chops with the melted butter for 1 minute.
5. Remove from heat and let the pork chops rest for 3-5 minutes before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook pork to an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C) and allow it to rest for at least 3 minutes before consuming.
