# Caprese Sandwich

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / gluten
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 10 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Ciabatta roll: 2 rolls
- Fresh mozzarella cheese: 200g
- Tomato: 2 medium
- Fresh basil leaves: 15g
- Balsamic glaze: 2 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 0.5 tsp
- Black pepper: 0.25 tsp

## Instructions
1. Slice the ciabatta rolls in half horizontally.
2. Slice the fresh mozzarella cheese and tomatoes into 1/4-inch thick rounds.
3. Layer the mozzarella, tomato slices, and fresh basil leaves on the bottom half of each roll.
4. Drizzle with balsamic glaze and olive oil, then season with salt and black pepper.
5. Place the top half of the roll on the sandwich and serve immediately.

## Safety Notes
- Handle the knife carefully when slicing the rolls, mozzarella, and tomatoes.
