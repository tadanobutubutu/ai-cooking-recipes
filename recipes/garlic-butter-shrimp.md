# Garlic Butter Shrimp

**⚠️ Allergens**: dairy / shellfish
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 15 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Shrimp (peeled and deveined): 300 grams
- Butter: 3 tbsp
- Garlic (minced): 1 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Lemon juice: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp
- Fresh parsley (chopped): 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil and 1 tbsp of butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Season the shrimp with salt and black pepper.
3. Add the shrimp to the skillet and cook for 1-2 minutes per side until pink and cooked through. Remove shrimp from the skillet and set aside.
4. Lower the heat to medium. Add the remaining 2 tbsp of butter to the skillet.
5. Once the butter is melted, add the minced garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
6. Stir in the lemon juice and remove from heat.
7. Return the shrimp to the skillet and toss in the garlic butter sauce until well coated.
8. Garnish with chopped fresh parsley before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Ensure shrimp is fully cooked; it should turn pink and opaque.
- Be careful not to burn the garlic, as it can turn bitter.
