# Fried Eggs

**⚠️ Allergens**: eggs
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 5 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Eggs: 200 grams
- Butter: 1 tbsp
- Salt: 1/4 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp

## Instructions
1. Heat the butter in a non-stick skillet over medium heat until melted and slightly bubbling.
2. Crack the eggs directly into the skillet.
3. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until the whites are set and the yolks are cooked to your desired doneness.
4. Season with salt and black pepper before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Wash hands after handling raw eggs to prevent contamination.
