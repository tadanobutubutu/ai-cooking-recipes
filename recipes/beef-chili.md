# Beef Chili

**⚠️ Allergens**: None
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 45 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Ground beef: 250g
- Onion (chopped): 100g
- Garlic (minced): 2 cloves
- Red bell pepper (chopped): 100g
- Kidney beans (canned, drained): 200g
- Diced tomatoes (canned): 400g
- Tomato paste: 2 tbsp
- Beef broth: 120ml
- Chili powder: 1 tbsp
- Cumin (ground): 1 tsp
- Oregano (dried): 1/2 tsp
- Salt: 1/2 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat.
2. Add chopped onion, minced garlic, and chopped red bell pepper to the pot. Sauté until the vegetables are softened, about 5 minutes.
3. Add the ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it apart with a spoon as it cooks. Drain any excess fat.
4. Stir in the chili powder, cumin, oregano, salt, and black pepper. Cook for 1 minute to toast the spices.
5. Add the drained kidney beans, diced tomatoes, tomato paste, and beef broth. Stir to combine.
6. Bring the chili to a simmer, then reduce the heat to low. Cover and simmer for at least 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
7. Serve hot, optionally topped with shredded cheese or sour cream (note: adding dairy toppings introduces a dairy allergen).

## Safety Notes
- Cook ground beef to an internal temperature of 160°F (71°C) to ensure it is safe to eat.
