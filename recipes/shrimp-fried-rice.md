# Shrimp Fried Rice

**⚠️ Allergens**: shellfish / eggs / soy
**🕐 Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
**👥 Servings**: 2

## Ingredients
- Cooked rice (preferably day-old): 300g
- Raw shrimp, peeled and deveined: 150g
- Eggs: 2
- Frozen mixed vegetables (peas and carrots): 100g
- Soy sauce: 2 tbsp
- Sesame oil: 1 tsp
- Vegetable oil: 2 tbsp
- Garlic, minced: 1 tsp
- Green onions, sliced: 2 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Heat 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Scramble the eggs in the pan, remove when just cooked, and set aside.
3. Add the remaining 1 tablespoon of oil to the pan. Add the minced garlic and shrimp. Cook until the shrimp turns pink, about 2-3 minutes.
4. Stir in the mixed vegetables and cook for 1 minute.
5. Add the cold, cooked rice to the pan, breaking up any clumps with a spatula.
6. Return the scrambled eggs to the pan. Pour the soy sauce and sesame oil evenly over the rice.
7. Stir-fry everything together for 3-5 minutes until the rice is heated through and the ingredients are well mixed.
8. Garnish with sliced green onions before serving.

## Safety Notes
- Cook shrimp to an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C). Ensure they are opaque and pink.
- Ensure leftover rice is stored properly and reheated thoroughly to 165°F (75°C) to prevent foodborne illness.
